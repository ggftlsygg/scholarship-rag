EIFFEL PROGRAMME
«
Developed and financed by the 
French Ministry of Foreign Affairs 
and managed by Campus France, 
the Eiffel scholarship programme 
is a source of success, prestige, 
knowledge and innovation. It aims to 
develop the attractiveness of French 
higher education and research 
institutions and assist talented 
international students in pursuing 
their studies in France. Recipients 
of the scholarship are pre-selected 
by their institution and awarded by a 
jury of experts.
