4. Recipients or categories of recipients
 Personal data may be shared with the following reci-
pients or categories of recipients:
4.1  Contract data processors
 We use service providers to process personal data 
on our behalf (so-called contract data processors, cf. 
Article 4 (8, 28) GDPR). These include the following 
companies or the service providers in the following 
categories:
- Service providers in the areas of IT (maintenance) 
and telecommunications
4.2 Responsible parties
 We share personal data with the following third par-
ties, which act as responsible parties in processing 
the data (Article 4(7) GDPR):
4.2.1 Union, liaison professor
 When you apply for support, the following personal 
information:
- last name, first name
- email address
- postal address
- place of study
 - course of study/fields of study
- number of semesters/information about PhD
- responsible funding unit
- liaison professor
- union membership
 is shared with the following so that the application 
process can be completed:
- the (executive, federal) board of the union to which 
you belong
- as applicable, the local Federation of German Trade 
Unions (DGB) group to which you belong
- your liaison professor (only in the case of applicants 
assigned to a liaison professor)
In keeping with the purpose of the Hans Böckler Founda-
tion under its charter, the relevant data are shared 
with third parties in order to review the application 
and reach a decision on whether the applicant will 
receive funding. This makes it possible to take the 
measures necessary prior to entering into a funding 
contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).
4.2.2 Preventing duplicate funding
 In order to prevent duplicate funding, the following 
personal information:
- name, including all given names
- address,
- date of birth
- location of university
 is shared with the following recipients so that inqui-
ries may be made and data may be compared:
- other institutions that provide support for talented 
individuals
- offices for educational support
- other institutions providing support
- Germany’s Federal Ministry of Education and Rese -
arch
 In this case, too, such information is shared so that 
a decision can be made regarding the application 
(Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).
4.2.3 Parties involved in the support provided by the Hans 
Böckler Foundation
 In addition, the information referred to in Section 
4.2.1 above may be shared with any person, enti -
ty or funding agency involved in the Foundation’s 
scholarships, provided that you have given your 
consent. Your consent provides the legal basis for 
sharing data (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). It may be revo -
ked at any time, effective going forward. Further de-
tails can be found in Section 9.3 below.
5. Transmitting data to a third country or an internatio-
nal organization
  As a matter of principle, we do not transmit your 
data to third countries (countries outside the Eu -
ropean Economic Area, EEA) or to international 
organizations. Under certain conditions, however, 
sharing information with a liaison professor may 
mean transmitting it to a third country if that person 
resides in a third country. Data is transmitted for the 
purpose of completing a contract to provide sup -
port, and this is therefore permissible under Article 
49(1)(b) GDPR.
6. Data retention period
 When you, on your own initiative, submit applicati -
on materials, we review them to determine whether 
support can be considered. Your information is re -
tained for the duration of the selection process. Per-
sonal information regarding applicants who are con-
sidered but ultimately not awarded a scholarship by 
the selection committee is retained for three years, 
based on the guidelines of the funding agency (Ge -
neral Auxiliary Conditions for Project Grants of the 
Federal Ministry of Education and Research), which 
are binding for the Hans Böckler Foundation, so that 
the funder is able to review the costs that have been 
incurred.
 If an applicant is awarded a scholarship, the relevant 
data is entered into the Hans Böckler Foundation’s 
database on support provided for undergraduate 
and postgraduate students. In this case the docu -
mentation and agreement concerning data protecti-
on law are handled separately.
 The data we process because of a legitimate interest 
is retained as long as that legitimate interest per -
sists.
7. Obligation to provide information
 In applying for a scholarship, you are required to 
provide the personal information we need to review 
your application and decide whether support will be 
granted, taking into account the conditions for sup-
port, as well as the information we are required by 
law to collect. Without this information, we will not 
be able to consider your application.
8.  Automated decision-making and profiling
 We do not use your personal information for auto -
mated decision-making, including profiling.

9.  Rights of the affected person
9.1 Information, correction, deletion, restrictions on 
processing, data transmissibility
 Any affected individual has the right to information 
under Article 15 GDPR, the right to correction under 
Article 16 GDPR, the right to deletion under Article 
17 GDPR, the right to restrict processing under Ar -
ticle 18 GDPR and the right to data transmissibility 
under Article 20 GDPR. In order to assert the rights 
listed above, you can contact the responsible parties 
listed under No. 1 above (Responsibility for data pro-
cessing and contact information).
 
9.2 Right of objection under Article 21 of the General 
Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
 For reasons related to your individual situation, you 
have the right at any time to object to the processing 
of your personal information based on Article 6(1)(f) 
of the GDPR(data processing based on a legitima -
te interest); this also applies to profiling within the 
meaning of Article 4(4) GDPR that is carried out on 
the basis of this provision. If you file an objection, 
we will no longer process your data unless we can 
show compelling and legitimate grounds for such 
processing that outweigh your interests, rights and 
freedoms, or unless the processing is for the purpo-
se of exerting, exercising or defending legal claims.
 If we process your personal data for the purpose of 
direct advertising, you have the right to file an ob -
jection at any time to such processing. If you object 
to the processing of your data for purposes of direct 
advertising, we will no longer process your personal 
data for these purposes.
 Your objection may be filed informally and should 
be addressed to the responsible parties listed under 
No. 1 above (Responsibility for data processing and 
contact information). 
9.3 Revoking consent
 If you have consented to the processing of your 
personal data, you may revoke that consent at any 
time. This does not affect the legality of the proces-
sing that has taken place, with your consent, up to 
the time at which you have revoked that consent.
10. Right to appeal to the supervisory authority
 Under Article 77 (1) GDPR, you have the right to lod-
ge a complaint with the supervisory authority if you 
believe that the processing of your personal infor -
mation has not occurred in a lawful manner, and in 
particular if it violates the GDPR.
