The Boustany MBA Cambridge Scholarship is granted once every two years for a one year course at Cambridge University, Judge Business School.
The Scholarship offers the following:
• Financial aid amounting 75% of the tuition fees
• Travel and accommodation expenses related to the internship.
The next Scholarship will be awarded for the class commencing Autumn 2027.

Candidates must have an excellent academic background and show considerable promise.
Candidates may apply for the Scholarship only after receiving an offer of admission from the Cambridge MBA programme.
Although the Scholarship can be awarded to candidates of any nation, priority will be given to candidates of Lebanese descent.
Should you wish to apply, please complete and submit your application directly though our online application form.
Please be sure to include all information requested, including a copy of your curriculum vitae with a photograph, GMAT scores and acceptance letter from the University.
Note that incomplete applications or those missing any of the required documents will not be considered.
If shortlisted, you will be invited to one or more interviews with the Foundation and one candidate will be awarded the Scholarship.
Deadline for the submission of candidacy: 31st May 2027.
The Scholarship will be awarded during the month of June 2027.
Over the summer, scholars are expected to complete a two-month unpaid internship in at our office in Monaco. Travel, food, and accommodation expenses related to the internship will be covered by the Foundation. The dates of the two-month period may be determined on a case-by-case basis between the scholar and the Foundation.
During the internship, scholars will work on either increasing awareness about the Foundation, designing or proposing new Scholarships, or working on other initiatives that further the vision of the Boustany Foundation. The exact project scope will be determined on the first day of the internship and can be driven by scholar interests as well.

What is included in the Cambridge University MBA Scholarship by the Boustany Foundation?
The scholarship covers 75% of the Cambridge MBA tuition fees and includes travel and accommodation expenses for the internship in Monaco. It is awarded to one candidate every two years for the one-year MBA at Cambridge Judge Business School.
Do Cambridge MBA scholars join a specific college at the University of Cambridge?
Yes. Scholarship recipients become members of Pembroke College, one of Cambridge’s oldest and most prestigious colleges, providing access to a strong academic and networking environment.
What does the Boustany Foundation internship involve for Cambridge MBA scholars?
Scholars must complete a two-month internship in Monaco, where they may work on projects such as:
- Raising awareness about the Foundation
- Proposing new scholarships
- Supporting strategic initiatives
The internship is tailored to the candidate’s interests, and all related expenses are covered.
When is the application deadline for the Cambridge MBA Scholarship?
The application deadline is the 31st of May 2027, with the scholarship awarded in June of the same year for the MBA intake starting in autumn.
Can international students apply for the Cambridge University MBA Scholarship?
Yes. The scholarship is open to candidates from all nationalities, although priority is given to applicants of Lebanese descent. Applicants must also first receive an admission offer from the Cambridge MBA program before applying.
