We’re here for you
The primary mission of the AAMU's Office of Student Financial Aid & Scholarships is to make a quality education both possible and affordable. We realize that a college education will be one of the most important investments a student and family will make. We do also realize that the financial aid process can sometimes be a daunting endeavor. Our goal is to provide our students and parents with the necessary information and guidance needed to make this process as simple and as manageable as possible.
