College and University Scholarships
Building a stronger talent pipeline of women in commercial real estate
The CREW Network Foundation scholarship program supports female students as they pursue higher education that will lead to careers in commercial real estate. In 2026, our U.S.-based foundation will grant a total of $300,000 USD—28 $5,000 undergraduate scholarships and 16 $10,000 graduate scholarships. CREW Network Foundation Canada will award up to five $5,000 CAD undergraduate scholarships and up to two $10,000 CAD graduate scholarships to Canadian residents.

Scholarship recipients receive:
- $5,000 (undergraduate) and $10,000 (graduate) awards for tuition and books
- Complimentary 24-month CREW Network student membership
- 2026 CREW Network Convention registration and a $1,000 travel stipend
- Six-month mentoring opportunity with a senior leader in commercial real estate
Scholarship Timeline
- Applications are open Jan. 5 – April 1
- Applications are reviewed in April and May
- Recipients are notified by phone in late June
- Applicants who did not receive the scholarship will be notified by email in late June
- In mid-July, a news release and email will publicly announce the scholarship recipients
