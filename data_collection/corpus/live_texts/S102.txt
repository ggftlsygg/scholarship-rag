At Clark University, we realize that enrolling in college is a significant commitment. Our financial aid team is here to guide you through the application process, honoring Clark’s core belief that education should be accessible to all.
Our team will work with you every step of the way to ensure that you understand the cost of a degree and, more importantly, are well-informed of the various forms of assistance available to you. We are dedicated to providing students and their families practical, useful, and objective guidance in their financial aid decisions.
Apply for financial aid
Financial aid is available for students and families of all income levels.
Types of aid
In addition to need-based financial aid, Clark University automatically considers all undergraduate applicants for merit-based scholarships. Special grants, loans, and student employment opportunities are also available.
What might you pay?
College is a significant investment that requires careful planning. Several tools are available to help you estimate the overall cost of your Clark education.
Reapply for financial aid
Current Clark students can renew their financial aid by completing the FAFSA and monitoring CUWeb for any additional requirements.
