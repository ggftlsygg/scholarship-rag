2023–2024 UNDERGRADUATE SCHOLARSHIPS
For questions regarding financial aid, contact Tommy Dismukes, College Affordability Adviser, at (334) 833-4402 or tdismukes@hawks.huntingdon.edu, or your admission counselor at (334) 833-4497 or by email at admission@hawks.huntingdon.edu.
- With the exception of the Montgomery Public Schools Investment Scholarship, institutional awards listed below cannot be stacked or combined.
- Institutional scholarships and awards are renewable for up to a minimum of three additional years, as long as award criteria are met.
- Federal, state, outside United Methodist scholarships and awards, and Veteran’s Administration Educational Assistance programs may be awarded in addition to institutional aid based on program qualifications.
- To be considered for a Huntingdon institutional scholarship or award, a student must be admitted to the traditional undergraduate program and must enroll as a full-time student.
- ACT/SAT composite scores are not necessary for admission but are necessary to be considered for Honors Scholarships and Presidential Fellowships and may be helpful for Merit Scholarship consideration.
