Welcome to the Lockheed Martin STEM Scholarship Program!
The 2024 STEM Scholarship Program application cycle is now closed. Lockheed Martin will not be offering new STEM scholarships in 2025 as we are exploring new ways to support post-secondary students in STEM. However, we are committed to supporting all our existing scholarship recipients and will continue to renew their scholarships in accordance with the eligibility requirements of our program. Please visit www.lockheedmartin.com/stem to learn more about our commitment to STEM education and current charitable investments.
