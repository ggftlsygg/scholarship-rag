About this MSc The financial sector is undergoing a paradigm shift. Investors and companies now require professionals who can analyze complex financial data while navigating sustainability regulations. Based in Barcelona, this Master equips you with the tools to lead this transformation. You will learn to use Artificial Intelligence and Big Data to audit accounts, manage risks, and create sustainable value for multinational organizations. This program is ideal for: 
 - • Finance & Economics Grads aiming for Green Finance.
 - • STEM Profiles applying data skills to finance.
 - • Aspiring Controllers looking to future-proof their careers.

Key Features FOCUSED ON FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT, NOT FINANCIAL MARKETS Unlike diverse finance masters that focus on trading or stock markets, we prepare you to manage the financial health and sustainability strategy of real companies. NO PROGRAMMING SKILLS REQUIRED – BUT YOU WILL LEARN THE TOOLS Don’t be intimidated by “Data Analytics.” No prior coding experience is needed. You will start from scratch and gain hands-on skills in Business Analytics, Python, R, and AI tools applied specifically to finance contexts. IDEAL FOR STUDENTS WITH OR WITHOUT ACCOUNTING BACKGROUND Whether you have a degree in Business, Economics, or Engineering, our curriculum is designed to bring everyone up to speed. Some knowledge of accounting is helpful, but not mandatory, as the program includes dedicated modules to build your financial analysis skills from the ground up. AI AND BUSINESS ANALYTICS AT THE CORE OF THE PROGRAM Data is the new currency of finance. You will learn how Artificial Intelligence and Big Data are revolutionizing the industry, mastering the techniques needed to drive strategic financial decisions in today’s data-driven business landscape. CFA CERTIFICATE IN SUSTAINABLE INVESTING – INCLUDED IN CURRICULUM Gain a competitive edge. The program includes a specific module to prepare you for the CFA Institute Certificate in ESG Investing. Our focus is on real-world application, management, and strategic decision-making. You will learn to use technology as a manager to solve business problems, not as a software engineer. Load More FAQ What is the main objective of the MSc Sustainable Financial Management? The MSc Sustainable Financial Management with Data Analytics is designed to train a new generation of financial professionals who can merge traditional corporate finance with ESG principles and data analytics. The program equips students to manage financial performance while driving positive social and environmental impact, combining technical finance skills with sustainability frameworks increasingly demanded by regulators and investors. Students learn to build financial models, assess risk, and interpret large datasets through an ESG lens, preparing them for roles in green finance, auditing, and financial controlling within companies, banks, and consulting firms committed to responsible, data-driven decision-making. WHY IS THIS PROGRAM RELEVANT IN TODAY'S BUSINESS WORLD? Sustainability and data analytics have become central to financial decision-making, as companies face growing regulatory pressure to report ESG performance and investors increasingly favor responsible portfolios. The MSc Sustainable Financial Management with Data Analytics responds directly to this shift, training students to bridge the gap between financial expertise and sustainability reporting standards such as CSRD and EU Taxonomy. In a job market where banks, asset managers, and auditing firms are actively recruiting ESG and sustainable finance specialists, this program gives graduates a clear competitive advantage by combining in-demand technical, analytical, and sustainability skills in one degree. WHAT IS THE DURATION OF THE PROGRAM AND WHERE IS IT LOCATED? The MSc Sustainable Financial Management with Data Analytics is taught 100% in English at our Barcelona campus. It offers two tracks depending on your background: a 1-year MSc (for students with a 4-year Bachelor’s) or a 2-year MSc (for students with a 3-year Bachelor’s). Both tracks are full-time., delivered at TBS Education’s Barcelona campus. Barcelona offers students an international, business-friendly environment with strong connections to the financial sector across Spain and Europe, alongside a high quality of life. Throughout the program, students benefit from small class sizes, hands-on case studies, and direct access to TBS Education’s network of partner companies in finance, auditing, and consulting, all while studying in one of southern Europe’s most dynamic financial and innovation hubs. WHAT DEGREE WILL I OBTAIN UPON COMPLETION? Graduates receive the MSc Sustainable Financial Management with Data Analytics, a French state-recognized Master’s degree (Diplôme d’Études Supérieures en Management Stratégique International), which is fully accredited by the French Ministry of Higher Education and Research Also, TBS Education, is a triple-accredited business school recognized internationally. This triple accreditation places the degree among a select group of business school qualifications worldwide, giving graduates strong credibility with employers in finance, auditing, and consulting. The diploma certifies both technical competency in financial management and data analytics, as well as specialized knowledge in ESG and sustainable finance practices increasingly required across the industry. DOES THE PROGRAM INCLUDE PROFESSIONAL CERTIFICATIONS? Yes. A key feature of this MSc is the dedicated preparation module for the CFA Institute’s Certificate in ESG Investing. This globally recognized certification demonstrates your expertise in sustainable investment to employers worldwide. Alongside the official MSc diploma, the program can incorporate industry-recognized certifications that strengthen students’ CVs and demonstrate practical mastery of key tools and frameworks used in sustainable finance and data analytics. These certifications complement the academic curriculum by validating specific technical skills sought by recruiters, giving graduates an additional credential that sets them apart when applying for roles in ESG analysis, sustainable investment, or financial data analytics. How is the program ranked? TBS Education is recognized as one of the few triple-accredited business schools worldwide, a distinction held by less than 1% of business schools globally, reflecting the quality of its programs including the MSc Sustainable Financial Management with Data Analytics.
