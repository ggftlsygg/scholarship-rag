Share your knowledge. Inspire project professionals worldwide.
PMI is powered by a global community of practitioners who contribute ideas, lessons learned, and real-world experiences. Whether you're submitting an article, webinar, template, or other resource, your expertise can help project professionals overcome challenges, discover new approaches, and achieve better outcomes.
We encourage contributors from all backgrounds and experience levels to participate. Please review the submission requirements carefully before submitting your content, as incomplete submissions may not be considered.
We look forward to helping you share your knowledge with the global PMI community.
To submit an article or template to ProjectManagement.com, we ask that you complete the following steps before submitting. These steps are to maximize your chances of success and ensure we can move your content quickly from submission to publication, if your article or template is accepted. Please make sure to complete the following:
- We ask all submitters to register and complete a free PMI profile. You can find the account creation page here. If you have any trouble creating your account, please reach out to support@pmi.org.
- Review the ProjectManagement.com article submission guidelines, found here.
- Review ProjectManagement.com's priority topics here. You are welcome to submit on any topic, but knowing which topics are a priority at this time may help your content fit into our editorial calendar more easily.
- Review our Top 20 Articles from 2025. Knowing what has been successful on ProjectManagement.com will help you understand the quality and style of writing our team is looking for. If you have any questions about this, please reach out to PMICommunityContent@pmi.org.
We encourage you to complete these steps before submitting your content to help you understand ProjectManagement.com's content better, recognize the type of content we are actively seeking, and make the submission process as easy as possible for our community members.
