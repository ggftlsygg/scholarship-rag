Residency
To help reduce the overall cost of attending Mizzou, many U.S. out-of-state students become Missouri residents to receive in-state tuition. Gaining residency is straightforward, and staff help students through the process.
The charts below provide an estimated total cost of attendance at Mizzou. The University of Missouri charges a flat tuition rate based on a student’s program of study, permitting a student to take 12-18 credits per term. The University of Missouri assesses tuition based on the student’s primary academic plan and their location (distance or on-campus). There are three tuition tiers for undergraduate students.
