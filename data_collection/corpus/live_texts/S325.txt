Real Estate and Planning African Scholarship in conjunction with the Reading Real Estate Foundation (RREF) and the African Real Estate Society (AfRES)
Open to offer holders for MSc Real Estate and MSc Real Estate Finance.

|---|---|

| Award value | £28,500 (full fee) | 
| Number available | Four, typically two for MSc Real Estate and two for MSc Real Estate Finance | 
| Closing date | 1st May 2026. All applications will be reviewed after the closing date and not on a rolling basis. Winners will be notified in June. | 
| Criteria: Applications for 2026 have now closed. Information about 2027 scholarships will be available soon. All Africa domiciled offer holders who have applied for the MSc Real Estate or MSc Real Estate Finance who have not previously studied outside their home country. Shortlisting and selection are based on academic merit and the candidates' responses to the questions in the application form. If offered a scholarship it must be accepted within 4 weeks of the scholarship offer date. After this deadline, scholarship offers may be withdrawn and reallocated to reserve candidates. | | 
| How to apply: Application is via the me@reading applicant hub once an offer has been made. You can only apply when you have received an offer (conditional or unconditional). When you view your offer in the hub you will see the link to apply for scholarship(s) relevant to your offer. You cannot apply via this website. | |
