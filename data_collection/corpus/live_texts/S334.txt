Funding and financial support
We understand that higher education is a significant financial investment in your future.
The University of Manchester is committed to ensuring equity of access and success for all our students, especially those who face finance-related barriers to participating and succeeding in higher education. We have a range of financial packages available for prospective and current students to support their overall university experience.
Our financial packages include scholarships, bursaries and grants to support tuition fees, course costs, work experience, extra-curricular activity and much more.
Our search tool lets you easily browse the support for which you are eligible. This tool does not yet include all Manchester’s schemes, or support for postgraduate or international students. Other schemes may be available by searching your Faculty or academic School webpages.
