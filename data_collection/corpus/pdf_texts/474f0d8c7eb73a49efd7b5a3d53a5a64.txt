Attention: Senior Class Counselors 
 
The Italian Catholic Federation (I.C.F.) is a National Roman Catholic Fraternal 
Organization composed of persons of Italian ancestry (including their non-Italian 
spouses) who are of the Roman Catholic Faith.   
 
One of the many programs of the Federation is to offer college scholarships to further 
the education of students who achieve a total cumulative GPA (3 ½ years) of at 
least 3.2 and meet all of the requirements in either #1 or #2:  1) Are Roman Catholic 
and of Italian Descent and live within the Roman Catholic Dioceses of California, 
Illinois (cities within the Archdiocese of Chicago only) and Reno in Nevada 
(which also includes the other cities within the Diocese of Reno), as these are 
the only Dioceses where Branches of the Federation are established.  or 2) if 
either Roman Catholic parent, guardian or grandparent is a member of the 
Italian Catholic Federation, the student must be Roman Catholic but need not be 
of Italian Descent or live where Branches of the Federation are established – 
membership of a Roman Catholic parent, guardian or grandparent is 
mandatory for #2.  *Please note the requirement of a parent or grandparent 
needing to be a member for Roman Catholic students who are not of Italian 
ancestry.  A student cannot just meet the requirement of being a baptized 
Roman Catholic. 
 
We invite you and interested students to go to our website at www.icf.org and 
download and complete the fillable scholarship application, attach the required 
documentation and submit the scholarship application via mail, fax or e-mail to our 
office.   Also available on the site, along with the application, is a flyer that can be 
posted to notify students of the availability of these scholarships.  If you or your 
students have any questions, you can call our office at 1-888-423-1924.  The amount 
of the scholarship is $500.00 and the application deadline is April 10, 2027. 
 
It is our belief that through your dedication to the education of our young people, and 
our interest in providing financial assistance to graduating seniors entering college, 
that together we can help our youth meet the challenges of tomorrow.
