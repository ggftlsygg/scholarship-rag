Career and Technical Student Organization Scholarship Application 
1 
 
 
Deadline to Apply: April 5, 2026 by 11:59 p.m. 
Open to high school seniors graduating in May 2026 and preparing to attend Mat-Su College 
of the University of Alaska Anchorage during the 2026-2027 academic year. Award waives 
24 credits of tuition applicable to Mat-Su College courses only (up to 12 credits each 
semester for Fall 2026 and Spring 2027). 
To be eligible, applicants must: 
1) Be a high school senior graduating in May 2026 and a member of an Alaska Career & 
Technical Student Organization (CTSO). These include BPA, DECA, FCCLA, EdRising, FFA, 
HOSA, & SkillsUSA. 
2) Have earned a minimum GPA of 2.75 or higher in high school 
3) Be registered or planning to register for classes at Mat-Su College for the fall 2026 
semester 
To apply: 
1) Apply for admission as an Undergraduate to any degree program offered by Mat-Su 
College 
2) Submit an official high school transcript. 
3) Complete the Career and Technical Student Organization (CTSO) Application form by the 
 
deadline: April 5, 2026, by 11:59 p.m.
