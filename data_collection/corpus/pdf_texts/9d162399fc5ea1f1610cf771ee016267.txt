 
 
A Joint Declaration by the Hatfield College Community 
 
We declare that we will abide by and actively promote our College ethos 
 
#AllOneHatfield 
 
reflecting our belief that a diverse, inclusive community of equals is of the highest value to all. 
 
We recognise that our ethos, which was born out of unequal times of the past, requires us to 
undertake constant self-reflection and pro-action to ensure that our College is fully welcoming, 
inclusive and celebratory of all our communities.  
 
For our ethos to reflect truly the lived experience of all our members, we promise that we will: 
 
 Persistently take action to ensure our College is accessible and welcoming to all 
 
 Endeavour to build a broad-based College community through pro-active recruitment, 
outreach and engagement 
 
 Celebrate together the many different cultures and ethnicities, histories, backgrounds and 
experiences of our members 
 
 Empower all our members to contribute fully to College life 
 
 Promote inclusion and educate all parts of our community to understand and value our 
differences and similarities 
 
 Guarantee that all parts of our community have voices which are heard and listened to 
 
 Make sure that all our communities are adequately represented through our leadership and 
decision-making forums and that the views and ideas of all are actively sought 
 
 Work continually to create an environment in which all our members feel safe and supported 
to voice concerns about any actions they experience or witness that are contrary to our ethos, 
and confident that their concerns will be pursued 
 
 Take appropriate action against any members who infringe our ethos 
 
This statement was written and agreed by representatives of all parts of our College: 
 
College Officers 
Junior Common Room 
Middle Common Room 
Senior Common Room 
College staff
