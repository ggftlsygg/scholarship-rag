THE FUTURE OF OUR YOUTH
BY THE NUMBERS
Through Community Grants, the Taco Bell 
Foundation supported more than 380 
charitable organizations in communities 
where Taco Bell does business. These 
charities create educational and career 
pathways for young people nationwide 
through mentorship, college and career 
exploration and readiness, financial literacy, 
entrepreneurship, and more. The Taco Bell 
Foundation has awarded over $166 million in 
grants to our charitable partners to date.
