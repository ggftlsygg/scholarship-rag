Raven McDonald Resilience Scholarship  
Raven McDonald, BSN, RN is a graduate from the illustrious Tuskegee University as of  
May 2020 with a degree in Bachelor of Science in Nursing. During her time at Tuskegee  
University, Raven became a member of Delta Sigma Theta Sorority, Inc., Chi Eta Phi Sorority, 
Inc., Miss Black and Gold, served as the captain of the Golden Essence Dance Team, and many 
other extracurricular activities and organizations.  
Raven is currently a travel nurse specializing in Cardiothoracic Stepdown Units. Raven 
understands the hard work, determination, and dedication that is required in the School of 
Nursing and Allied Health Program. She has created this scholarship to provide encouragement 
and financial support to undergraduate sophomore, junior, and senior Nursing and Health 
Science students, as well as graduate Occupational Therapy (OT) students. Raven understands 
how diligent students must work to obtain a professional degree in healthcare; therefore, she 
wants to promote resilience in students to achieve their goal. With this scholarship, Raven wants 
to influence students to keep going by overcoming the difficulties they may face while striving to 
obtain their degree. This scholarship award is in the amount of $500 each for five students in 
the Nursing, Health Science, or Occupational Therapy Program at Tuskegee University.  
Raven wants to acknowledge and give thanks to the sponsors who have contributed to 
this scholarship. The sponsors believe in investing in the future of education and empowering 
students to achieve their academic and career goals without a heavy financial burden. In addition, 
these sponsors are nursing and OT graduates from Tuskegee University.

Scholarships Requirements   
  
Each applicant must meet the following criteria:   
  
1. Current enrollment in Tuskegee University School of Nursing and Allied Health as a 
nursing student or occupational therapy student.   
  
2. Good scholastic standing at the time of application. Cumulative GPA of 3.00 or above on 
a 4.00 scale  
  
  
The application packet must contain all the following requirements:   
  
1. Official transcript(s) from Tuskegee University. Send all official transcripts 
electronically to ravenm.resiliencescholars@gmail.com   
  
2. A copy of your resume  
  
3. An essay that adheres to the essay guidelines   
  
4. One (1) letter of recommendation. One letter should come from a current faculty or dean 
that is written on the school's letterhead stationery. (You are not required to obtain a 
second letter of recommendation, but if you wish, you may request one from someone of 
your choice).   
  
  
Please note: Failure to submit a full and completed application packet with all the required 
documents will not be considered.  
  
  
  
  
  
  
 
  
 
5  
  
Essay Guidelines  
  
Scholarship applicants must pick at least one out of the three questions listed below to 
include in their essay. The following information must be included to be considered for the 
scholarship:  
  
1. Describe a time when you had to overcome a stressful situation within the nursing, OT, or 
health science program? What lessons did you learn? What skills did you develop and 
continue to implement to become a better person within your studies and life?  
  
2. How will you use your degree to improve the health status or social conditions of the 
community?  
  
3. What is your five-year goal after obtaining your degree from Tuskegee University School 
of Nursing and Allied Health?  
  
• Typed 12 font, non-Italic, and Times New Roman  
  
• Doubled spaced with one-inch margins.  
  
• Length: minimum one page, maximum three pages  
  
• Exhibits scholarly writing that is free of grammatical, spelling, and punctuation errors.  
  
  
  
The completed application packet must be emailed prior to or no later than April 20, 2026. 
Incomplete or late applications will not be considered.
