ICMS Professional Scholarships 
 
ICMS Professional Scholarships are awarded to a number of successful applicants each 
February. All scholarships cover partial tuition fees for study and/or accommodation at the 
discretion of the Scholarship Board’s selection. The value of all ICMS Professional 
Scholarships awarded will be advised to the scholarship winner at the time of being selected.

Corporate Partner Scholarships 
The ICMS Scholarship Program is partnered with several highly esteemed organisations. They contribute to the 
development of recipients through financial support for tuition and developmental support through work experience. 
This is unique, it provides unequalled endorsement for the successful recipients placing them at a precipice for success. 
 
Finalists for the ICMS Professional Scholarships will have opportunities to progress to become finalists for one of these 
sought after corporate partner scholarships.

application and selection processes. Students who receive this scholarship receive ceremonial recognition and honour 
board posterity. Our partners further enhance this honour, providing unique opportunities for successful applicants”

Applying for a Scholarship with ICMS 
• The scholarships listed above are open to Australian and New Zealand citizens/permanent residents completing 
or have successfully completed year 12 (NZ: year 13) or have taken a gap year in 2026. 
• Applicants must ensure they have submitted a Direct Application and returned their signed Acceptance 
Declaration to be considered for an ICMS Scholarship. 
• By completing this application, you will be considered for the ICMS Professional Scholarship*. 
• For all scholarship applications, the presentation, content, relevance and creativity of the application and the 
academic merit of applicants will be considered. 
• Applications close at 5PM on Friday 20 November 2026*. 
• Late applications will not be accepted. 
• Applications will be accepted via email or in hard copy. 
• Applications will not be returned to the applicant, please do not include any original documentation and make 
copies of all references. 
• Applications will be reviewed by the Scholarship Board led by the Chairperson of the Professional Scholarships 
Program. 
• Finalists will be chosen based on Stages 1 and 2 of this application. 
• Finalists will be required to sit a minimum of 2 rounds of interviews and must be available in person for each of 
these. (Refer Stage 3 information on the following page) 
• Scholarship winners will be chosen from the group of finalists. 
• It is important for students that have registered with UAC to place ICMS as their highest possible preference. 
 
* Where your chosen Bachelor degree is applicable to the scholarship available.

Application Process 
 
STAGE 1 - REFERRAL 
 
As an applicant, you must include a referral, or referrals from a school principal, career advisor or dean of studies (or 
similar). The referee must complete the attached form including a written reference with information about your 
personal qualities, academic ability, achievements in the school or wider community and any other relevant 
information. 
 
Please ensure the attached Referral Form is complete and signed by your referee. 
 
STAGE 2: REPORT 
 
Please submit a detailed report addressing the following points. (Minimum 500 words) 
1. How will you personally benefit from an education at ICMS? 
2. Outline your experience in working in teams and how you intend to apply that experience during your time at 
ICMS. 
3. Consider your career goal for the future. You have graduated from ICMS and imagine you are in a 
management role. Describe your position and explain how you hope your experiences at ICMS have helped 
you reach this level. 
4. Identify a successful organisation in your chosen field of study in which you would like to work. 
Explain this organisation’s key business activities and position within its market sector. Explain why you would 
want to work for this company. 
If you were provided a work placement opportunity with this company as part of an ICMS scholarship, describe 
where in this company you would like to work. 
Describe three things you would like to learn during your placement. 
 
 
TIPS… 
• Visit www.icms.edu.au 
• Be creative and realistic 
• Interviews with industry professionals may assist you with developing your ideas 
 
The following documents must also be submitted with the report. 
• Last set of high school transcripts and/or trial results 
• School and community awards and/or leadership roles 
• References (personal and/or work) 
• ICMS Application form (if submitted online, please print a copy of the submission OR your Letter of Offer 
received by the Admissions department)

STAGE 3: INTERVIEW 
Based on the results of Stages 1 & 2 a list of finalists will be selected for a first-round interview by the Scholarship 
Board. 
The interview will be conducted at the ICMS campus in Manly, NSW, and will be used to further assess the candidate.  
 
Round One Interviews are scheduled from Wednesday 25 November to Friday 27 November 2026*. Please ensure you 
are available during this time period. 
 
After completion of first round interviews the Scholarship Board will select finalists based on their suitability relative 
to the range and specifics of the ICMS Professional Scholarships including corporate partner scholarships.  
 
The successful short-listed finalists will progress to Round Two interviews held Monday 07 December to Friday 11 
December 2026* and your availability should be prioritised. 
 
 
STAGE 4: ACADEMIC 
 
The final scholarship recipients will be selected based on the results of Stages 1 through 3 and final Year 12 
(NZ: Year 13) results. All interviews will be held on campus at ICMS, 151 Darley Road, Manly, NSW. 
 
If you are selected as a finalist, you will be required to submit your final Year 12 results to the attention of the 
Scholarship Board within 48 hours of receipt by email to icmsscholarships@icms.edu.au 
 
*Dates may be subject to change. 
 
 
NOTE: Scholarship submissions will not be returned. Unsuccessful applications are not retained and will be securely destroyed.

Terms & Conditions ( please read carefully before signing the declaration) 
 
1. All scholarships awarded are subject to approval and final selection by the final ICMS Scholarships Chair and panel and this decision is 
final. 
2. ICMS or the participating company reserves the right to terminate the ICMS Professional Scholarship (the 
Scholarship) at any time for poor academic performance or inappropriate conduct (see Student Code of Conduct). 
3. College fees are subject to change. 
4. The Scholarship is not refundable and non-transferrable, meaning the Scholarship cannot be converted to another ICMS 
course^, education provider, or recipient nominated by the original recipient. 
5. Once the successful recipient has been notified of the scholarship value, that value is not negotiable and not 
changeable. 
6. The Scholarship is not available to students using any other scholarship, bursary or discount at ICMS*. 
7. The scholarship amount awarded will be credited to tuition as a portion of the fees in each study period for the duration of the 
course of their study or until the scholarship credit is exhausted. Scholarship credit can and may be applied to a later study period 
than the commencement study period. 
8. If the recipient’s scholarship involves a work placement and a tuition credit component, the tuition credit will apply to 
the next term of study. At no time will any scholarship credit apply to fees already paid. 
9. Previous scholarship recipients cannot have existing scholarship credit remaining as at the commencement of the study 
period they are applying for. A recipient can hold only one scholarship at any given time or any form of financial subsidy. 
10. The recipient is aware that a High Academic Place (HAP) is of no greater value than the Scholarship and an acceptance of the 
Scholarship supersedes a HAP. 
11. The applicant must ensure they have submitted a Direct Application and returned their signed Acceptance Declaration 
to be considered for an ICMS Scholarship. 
12. The recipient of the Scholarship agrees to abide by all admissions criteria, rules, regulations and all relevant policies 
prescribed by ICMS while enrolled. 
13. The recipient of the Scholarship must commence the program in the February 2027 study period and is not able to defer 
study at any time during his/her scholarship. It is the responsibility of the recipient to contact the Admissions Manager 
at the ICMS to apply for commencement within the specified period. 
14. If the recipient of the Scholarship fails a subject, the recipient is obliged to retake the subject at their own cost. 
15. ICMS Scholarship students can apply for exchange programs offered through ICMS. Any payments to be paid to the 
exchange partner are the liability of the student and are not covered under the Scholarship conditions. 
16. The Scholarship is a tuition waiver that can only be used for the purpose of attending ICMS for the program as specified. 
The balance of remaining fees, including accommodation, uniforms, books and any other program related costs for 
each enrolling study period must be paid in full prior to study period commencement**.
