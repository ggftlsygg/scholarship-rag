Emissions reduction targets 
We project that carbon emissions will decrease over the next five years to at least 1167.39 
tCO2e by 2027. This is a reduction of 11.98 %.  
In order to continue our progress to achieving Net Zero, we have implemented the following 
carbon reduction action plans:  
• We have reduced the requirement to commute to the office from 100% to 40% over a 
month.   
• We have reduced international travel to ensure fewest staff possible attend a 
particular event. 
• We have reduced our print volume by introducing digital publication and digital 
transfer of documents such as invoices and internal/external communications.

Carbon Reduction Projects 
Completed Carbon Reduction Initiatives 
The following environmental management measures and projects have been completed or 
implemented since the 2023 baseline. Measures have been taken prior to 2023 to support 
the carbon emission reduction initiative.  
Some of the carbon reductions projects ACU has been involved in, includes the following:  
• Crown Commercial Services PPN 06/21 - Carbon Reduction Plan creation and 
training 
• Registration on deep dive emissions webinar by Sustain Life to understand scope of 
carbon emissions and detailed requirements, in additional to the existing government 
technical guidance.  
• We have recruited an internal Compliance Manager to oversee and review the 
carbon reduction scheme in line with the government requirements and mission.  
• We have maintained 100% renewable electricity contracts with our landlord.  
• We have run a communications campaign and ongoing awareness meetings with 
relevant stakeholders within ACU, to discuss how to achieve net zero and contribute 
towards this together.  
• We are in the process of developing ESG e-learning with our colleagues and we 
have already discussed the definition of Net zero with Senior Management Team and 
trustees.  
• Begun consultation with stakeholders to establish a target date before which we will 
aim to reach Net Zero in line with the SBTi corporate net zero standard. 
• We have Senior Leadership oversight of the agenda who are currently reviewing and 
finalising our materiality assessment with the Strategic Leadership Team. 
In the future we hope to implement further measures such as: 
 
• Continuously work to collect the highest quality, primary data on our emissions to aid 
in reduction and progress measurement. 
 
• Work with key individuals on carbon reduction plans such as setting carbon budgets 
and working with our third parties to reduce emissions to achieve net zero in line with 
the SBTi corporate net zero standard.  
 
• Reduced our in person Council meeting from 2 to 1 per year thus reducing air milage 
for Council members to fly to London. 
 
• Continue to procure 100% renewable electricity for owned sites.  
 
• Reduce business travel by air, rail, and road through effective measurement and a 
sustainable travel policy. 
 
• Work with suppliers/third parties to understand their own emissions and encourage 
them to set their own carbon reductions targets in line with the science. 
 
 
 
Declaration and Sign Off 
This Carbon Reduction Plan has been completed in accordance with PPN 06/21 and 
associated guidance and reporting standard for Carbon Reduction Plans. 
Emissions have been reported and recorded in accordance with the published reporting 
standard for Carbon Reduction Plans and the GHG Reporting Protocol corporate standard1 
and uses the appropriate Government emission conversion factors for greenhouse gas 
company reporting2. 
Scope 1 and Scope 2 emissions have been reported in accordance with SECR 
requirements, and the required subset of Scope 3 emissions have been reported in 
accordance with the published reporting standard for Carbon Reduction Plans and the 
Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard3. 
This Carbon Reduction Plan has been reviewed and signed off by the board of directors (or 
equivalent management body).
