Scholarship Announcement  
The purpose of this scholarship is to award one student (rising Junior or Senior) from the Construction 
Science and Management Department or College of  Business and Information Sciences who has 
achieved academic excellence, demonstrates revolutionary leadership with integrity, understands their 
obligation to the community, and to support the fight for the underprivileged.  
Your award is subject to your enrollment at Tuskegee University as a full-time student (12 credits or more) 
and you must abide by the Academic Polices of Tuskegee University. The candidates who will be considered 
for the award must be entering their junior or senior year at Tuskegee University. Please be advised that a 
student can only be awarded this scholarship once during their tenure at Tuskegee University.   
The amount of the scholarship will be $3,500 each academic year. Please note that your scholarship award 
will be divided equally for both semesters. The minimum cumulative GPA for the scholarship consideration 
will be 3.0. However, the recipient of the award must maintain a 3.0 during the fall semester of the 
scholarship in order for the award to continue during the spring semester.
