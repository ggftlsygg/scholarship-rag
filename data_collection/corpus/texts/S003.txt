The Boustany Foundation is pleased to offer Cambridge University MBA Scholarships to qualified international students seeking MBA degrees at the prestigious in the United Kingdom. The Cambridge University MBA Scholarship is offered once every two years and it covers the tuition fees of its selected applicants. The scholarship also offers post-graduation internship as well as travel and accommodation expenses related to the internship. In this post, Scholarship region explains the full details of the Cambridge University Scholarship in simple terms. You will also be provided with information regarding the eligibility criteria, benefits, and the steps to follow to submit your application successfully. Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: 75% Tuition | Accommodation | Flight Costs Deadline: May 15, Cambridge University MBA Scholarship Summary The Cambridge MBA is one of the world’s leading business programmes, taught at one of the world’s most prestigious universities. The Cambridge University MBA Scholarship recipients become members of Pembroke College, the third oldest and one of the most highly regarded colleges. The Boustany MBA Cambridge Scholarship is granted once every two years for a one year course at Cambridge University, Judge Business School. Cambridge University MBA Scholarship Requirements: To be eligible for the Cambridge University MBA Scholarship, Candidates must have an excellent academic background and show considerable promise. Candidates may apply for the Scholarship only after receiving an offer of admission from the Cambridge MBA programme. Although the Scholarship can be awarded to candidates of any nation, priority will be given to candidates of Lebanese descent. Benefits of Cambridge University MBA Scholarship The Scholarship offers the following: Financial aid amounting 75% of the tuition fees Successful scholars are expected to complete a two-month unpaid internship with the Foundation. Travel and accommodation expenses related to the internship are covered by the Foundation.
