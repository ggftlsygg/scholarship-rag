The David Sainsbury Scholarship offers students from developing countries the prestigious opportunity for a Master’s degree at the University of East Anglia. The David Sainsbury Scholarship will cover the full tuition fees (£33,150) for selected applicants, provide a living stipend of £18,622, and an additional £4,000 for travel costs. This post will feed you the details of the scholarship in full, its requirements, and the step-by-step processes involved in the application.
