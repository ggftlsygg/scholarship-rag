Applications for the Eira Francis Davies Scholarship are now open for students from developing countries with outstanding academic performance who are interested in studying in the United Kingdom. The Francis Davies Scholarship is a full tuition fee scholarship ordinarily awarded to an outstanding student per academic year. In this post, we will explain the details of the Francis Davis Scholarship. We will also discuss its eligibility requirements, an the step-by-step application procedure. Eira Francis Davies Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: Developing countries Reward: Full Scholarship Deadline: May 26, Scholarship Alerts: To receive Scholarship Alerts on WhatsApp, Click HERE Eira Francis Davies Scholarship Details: The Eira Francis Davies (EDS) Full Fees Scholarship is a full tuition fee scholarship ordinarily awarded to one outstanding female student per academic year. The student must be a national of and resident in an eligible developing country, who pursues a postgraduate taught Master’s programme within the Faculty of Medicine, Health and Life Science Previous Eira Francis Davies Scholarship recipients have come from a range of different academic backgrounds and have travelled from many different countries. Eira Francis Davies Scholarship Requirements : To be considered for the Eira Francis Davies Scholarship (EFD) full fees scholarship, you must:hold a minimum 2:1 bachelor’s degree or equivalent qualification. have formally accepted an offer to study at Swansea University in September : be female. be a new student to Swansea University from September . within their application, demonstrate strong academic ability, financial plan to study and motivation for their chosen course at Swansea University. be a national, and permanent resident of an EFD Eligible Countries as listed by the World Bank Country and Lending Groups classification as being ‘low income’ or ‘lower-middle income’ as of 1 July . not receive any other financial support from Swansea University (e.g. tuition fee reduction, scholarship or bursary). not be a progressing student from The College, Swansea University.
