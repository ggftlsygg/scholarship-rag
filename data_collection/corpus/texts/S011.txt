The Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarships (QECS) offer a unique opportunity to study a two-year Master’s degree in a low or middle income Commonwealth country. The Queen Elizabeth Scholarship is fully funded; it covers the full tuition, accommodation, living stipends, travel costs, as well as a one-off arrival allowance for all successful applicants. In this post, Scholarship Region will highlight for you in simple terms, the details, requirements, benefits, and the method of application for the Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarship. Eligibility Requirements for Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarship To apply for the Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarship: The candidate must be a citizen of a Commonwealth country. Must hold an undergraduate degree at the time of admission. No age limit or restriction. Each university may have specific eligibility requirements for admissions. Benefits of Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarship The Scholarship period will run for 2 years with the following benefits: Fully-funded tuition fees Living allowance (stipend) for duration of award Return economy flights to host country One-off arrival allowance Research Support Grant (on request only; subject to approval) Required Documents for Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarship The application form of the Queen Elizabeth Commonwealth Scholarship consists of the following sections: Eligibility Personal Details Academic & Employment Record Course at host university details Statements Upload supporting documents (including references)
