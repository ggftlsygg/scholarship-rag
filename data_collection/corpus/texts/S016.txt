The University of Bristol invites applications for the University of Bristol Think Big Undergraduate and Postgraduate Scholarships. The aim of this scholarship is to support the postgraduate study of overseas students. All International Students from all over the world are eligible to apply for the Think Big Scholarships at the University of Bristol. Aspirants who want to pursue their Bachelor’s Degree or Master’s Degree from the UK are encouraged to apply for the award. There is No Application Fee to apply. University of Bristol Think Big Scholarships Summary Host Country: United Kingdom (UK) Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Undergraduate Scholarships | Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: £13,000 – £26,000 No IELTS Required Deadline: April 29, University of Bristol Think Big Scholarships Details: The Think Big Scholarship scheme offers tuition fee scholarships of up to £13,000 to £26,000 per year study, as well as one-off living cost bursaries of up to £3,000 to support prospective international students at the University of Bristol. Eligibility Requirements for University of Bristol Think Big Scholarships To be considered for the University of Bristol Think Big Scholarships: For Undergraduate Degree Applicants must be considered an overseas fee payer and have applied to an eligible fulltime Undergraduate course at the University of Bristol, beginning in September . All courses with the exception of Medicine, Dentistry and Veterinary Science are eligible. Please note that scholarship applicants can submit an application before receiving a formal offer to study from the University, but they need to have at least applied to an eligible course For Masters Degree: Applicants must be considered an overseas fee payer and have applied to an eligible fulltime postgraduate course at the University of Bristol, beginning in September . any one-year taught Master’s programme, or one of the following two-year taught Master’s: MA Law or MSc Social Work. Distance learning programmes are ineligible. Please note that scholarship applicants can submit an application before receiving a formal offer to study from the University, but they need to have at least applied to an eligible programme and be awaiting an outcome Benefits of University of Bristol Think Big Scholarships Think Big scholarship awards are applied to tuition fees only for every year that an awardee is in full time study. In addition to scholarship awards, awardees may receive a living cost bursary of up to £3,000.
