 University of Edinburgh Global Scholarship
https://www.ed.ac.uk/student-funding/undergraduate/international/mathematics

The University of Edinburgh Global Scholarship is awarded to applicants with International fee status who are accepted for full-time admission to an undergraduate degree programme hosted in the School of Mathematics at the University. The scholarship is not available to students already on programme. It is also not available to students studying Mathematics as part of a degree programme hosted by another School within the University.
