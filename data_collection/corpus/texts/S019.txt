The University of Michigan High Potential Scholarship is designed to support 150 exceptional international students seeking undergraduate, master’s, or doctoral degrees. The High Potential Scholarship is a $35,000 annual award offered to cover tuition fee and monthly stipend each academic year to highly talented students from outside the United States. This post contains a detailed breakdown of the application process, requirements and benefits of the High Potential Scholarship. The scholarship is aimed at helping students who have been admitted to an Undergraduate, master’s, PhD programme at the University of Michigan. The Scholarship programme consists of both the High Potential Scholarship (funded by the University Scholarship Fund), and the Albright Scholarship, which is financed by the Vanderbilt Institute in conjunction with J.Morgan Grant.
