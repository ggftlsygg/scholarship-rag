Allan and Nesta Ferguson Scholarship In UK (University of East Anglia)

The Allan and Nesta Ferguson Scholarship offers £29,997.75 each to winners covering a full-time one-year Master’s course in the School of Global Development at UEA.Each scholarship will fund the student’s international tuition fees and provide them with a maintenance grant of £12,000 so that they can live in the UK and be able to cover the costs of living/ travel/ visa.
