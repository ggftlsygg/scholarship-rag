ACTIVE STUDENT SCHOLARSHIP The AUS Active Student Scholarship offered by the Office of Student Affairs is aimed at providing scholarships to outstanding active students who demonstrate excellence in extracurricular activities and whose efforts yield exceptional results and inspire other students to become high achiever Criteria: Undergraduate student with a minimum of 12 credit hours in the semester that the application is submitted Minimum CGPA of 2.5 Achievements in AUS extracurricular activities for Spring 2024 and Fall 2023 (virtual engagement included). Those who have been actively involved in interest-oriented and cultural clubs, community service volunteers, leadership groups, Student Council, academic associations and societies, and athletics and recreation are encouraged to apply. Good moral character with no disciplinary action on record Conditions: The scholarship entitles the recipient to an award of up to 25 percent tuition remission for one semester. Scholarship recipients must be registered for a minimum of 12 credit hours during the semester they are receiving the scholarship.
