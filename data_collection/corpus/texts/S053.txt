American university of sharjah President’s Scholarship
https://www.aus.edu/admissions/financial-grants-and-scholarships#bachelor-s--grants-and-scholarships

In its efforts to attract academically qualified and talented applicants from outside and within the UAE, the university plans to introduce competitive scholarships to various cohorts. Three categories of scholarships will be offered starting Fall 2024: President’s Scholarship The President’s Scholarship is open to applicants from outside the UAE and covers 100 percent of tuition and lab fees and an application fee waiver, offers a monthly stipend for the duration of the scholarship, includes on-campus housing, three round-trip tickets for awardees during their years of study, and student medical insurance. It will be offered for qualifying first-time, first-year students who will enroll at AUS in the fall semester. The selection process depends heavily on academic excellence and a series of other requirements that showcase students’ achievements. The scholarship will be renewable for students who continue to maintain the scholarship requirements. Academic requirements: British qualification: 3 A-levels & 5 GCSEs(All A grades) IB Diploma: Minimum 40 points American curriculum: Minimum 98% with 3 APs at grade 5 or SAT combined score of 1400 – Weighted average Others: Equivalent to above requirements IELTS 7.0 or TOEFL iBT 94 Additional requirements: Applicants to submit a portfolio to include: An essay detailing extracurricular activities, talents, social activities and anticipated contribution to AUS Evidence of outstanding personal qualities and leadership abilities in schools/community Evidence of scientific or literary contributions to the community School honors/awards, community service and school extracurricular activities Submit a 3 to 5 minute video describing why they should be considered for the scholarship Two letters of recommendation Registration status: Minimum of 15 credit hours/semester (fall and spring) Other conditions: No student conduct code violations that result in conduct probation or greater sanctions No academic integrity code violations that result in a sanction Applicants will be evaluated based on their grade 10, 11, and predicted grades for the final year (where applicable). The conditional scholarship offer will be revoked for those not meeting the predicted grades in the final year. They can be considered for the merit scholarship and/or financial grants.
