Anhui Medical University CSC Scholarship
https://studyinchina.csc.edu.cn/

Applications for the Anhui Medical University CSC Scholarship 2024-2025 are open for international students. It is a fully funded program which is offered by the Government of China. You can undertake the Master’s or Doctoral degree at Chinese Anhui Medical University. You can apply for this scholarship without any fee. Anhui Medical University is a major provincial university in Hefei, Anhui Province, China. The university has 16,673 students. Applicants can study MS and PHD programs in both languages Chinese or English. Details Country: China Organization: Anhui Medical University Degree: you can obtain MS or PHD Who can apply? Students of all nationalities. Benefits provided by the scholarship This scholarship offers many different benefits, which you can learn about below: Full Tuition Fee Waiver Accommodation, Double Room for Master, Single Room for PhD Monthly Stipend: For Master and General Scholars RMB 3,000/month, For PhD and Senior Scholars RMB 3,500/month. Comprehensive medical insurance 800 yuan/year. Documents required to apply for the Scholarship You must prepare the following documents to use in applying for this grant. The required documents are: The application form must be written in Chinese or English Passport Copy Notarized highest education certificate. Documents in languages ​​other than Chinese or English must be accompanied by a notarized Chinese or English translation. Academic transcripts A copy of the Pre-admission Letter (if available). A copy of the HSK Certificate (if available). A Study Plan or Research Proposal (minimum of 200 words for undergraduate, 500 words for non-degree students, 800 words for graduate students). Two Recommendation Letters signed by professors or associate professors. Valid documents of your legal guardians in China (only for those under the age of 18). A copy of the Foreigner Physical Examination Form Eligibility Criteria International applicants except Chinese nationals are eligible for this scholarship. Applicants must be in good health. Applicants must have a good educational background Applicants must have a bachelor’s degree for master’s degree studies and be under the age of 35. Applicants must have a master’s degree for doctoral degree studies and be under the age of 40. There is no way to combine this scholarship with another one.
