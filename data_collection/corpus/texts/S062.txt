The Australian National University RTP Scholarship provides funding to Universities to award scholarships to domestic and international students undertaking Higher Degree Research programs. The Australian National University Scholarship covers the full tuition costs of selected candidates including a stipend of $35, 652 for personal, thesis, and child/dependent allowance. Australian National University RTP Scholarship Details: Each year the Commonwealth Department of Education and Training provides funding to Universities to award scholarships to domestic and international students undertaking Higher Degree Research programs. ANU currently conducts two annual central scholarship rounds which includes the Australian National University RTP Scholarship, the University Research Scholarship and a few other centrally managed scholarships. Candidates are ranked and scholarships are awarded based on a competitive ranking process, to the top ranked candidates, subject to evidence they meet merit-based selection criteria, and subject to confirmation of a qualified supervisor, space and resources being available, in an area of research that is aligned with ANU research strategy. Eligibility Requirements for Australian National University RTP Scholarship 2024 To be considered for the Australian National University RTP Scholarship, you must: Be a domestic or international student Be applying to study a Higher Research Degree (HDR) Master’s or PhD programme Meet the entry requirements for your chosen programme Meet the Engish Language proficiency requirements of the University of Sydney.
