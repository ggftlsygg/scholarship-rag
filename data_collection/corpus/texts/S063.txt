The Secretariat of Science and Technology informs that the evaluation process for applications for Postgraduate Scholarships, for Master's or Doctorate, and for Postdoctoral Scholarships has ended. IMPORTANT: The applicant for Postgraduate Scholarships, for Master's and Doctorate, who at the time of submitting the application was not a graduate, must graduate by 03/31/ and present by email a certificate of graduation or sworn statement before 5/4/ to be able to access the Scholarship. Likewise, the applicant for Postdoctoral Scholarships who at the time of submission of the application did not have a doctorate, must defend the thesis until 03/31/ and present proof of the defense of the thesis until 4/5/ . to be able to access the Postdoctoral Scholarship. Applicants who have been proposed to be awarded the corresponding scholarship will be notified by email. Each one must communicate their acceptance or rejection of the scholarship via email within 72 hours of receiving the communication.
