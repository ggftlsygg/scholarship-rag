Bilkent University International Scholarships

Bilkent University welcomes you to an exceptional learning environment that will fulfill your academic ambitions at the undergraduate and postgraduate levels. In line with our founding motto, “excellence in teaching and research,” Bilkent University believes graduate education plays a central role in a research university. As such, from the beginning Bilkent has allocated, and continues to devote, the scholarships and resources necessary to create a fertile environment for advanced study and research. Bilkent University International Scholarships Summary: Host Country: Turkey Study Abroad: Study in Asia Category: Undergraduate Scholarships | Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships | PhD Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Tuition waiver | Monthly Stipend. Bilkent University International Scholarships Details: Bilkent University aims to inform prospective undergraduate and graduate students about the many outstanding study opportunities at the University. Bilkent University offers admission to applicants who have the necessary qualifications and show promise of scholarly achievement. Each department establishes its own criteria for admission, and departmental listings include more detailed information about these requirements. Eligibility Requirements for 2024 Bilkent University International Scholarships: To be considered for the Bilkent University International Scholarships, applicant must: have a minimum of 2.80/4.00 CGPA for BSc graduates or MSC students at the time of application. If you are a Turkish citizen resident in Turkey: ALES score is required, <55 (quantitative) Otherwise; GRE General Test score is required: 153 (quantitative). However, if you are off by a small margin and you have a strong background, we encourage you to apply. TOEFL IBT(at least 87), IELTS (at least 6.5 average and 5.5 from each section) or Bilkent COPE (at least C). All nationality are eligible to apply Benefits of 2024 Bilkent University International Scholarships: Financial assistance is provided in the form of a tuition waiver or a tuition waiver plus a monthly stipend. The amount of the stipend is adjusted annually to cover changes in the cost of living. Required Documents for Bilkent University International Scholarships: To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; Submission of ALES (Akademik Personel ve Lisansüstü Eğitimi Giriş Sınavı/Academic Personnel and Postgraduate Education Entrance Exam) or GRE scores. GMAT scores are required for graduate programs in Business Administration.) Fluency in written and oral English. Please see the websites of each graduate school for the list of accepted tests. Official transcripts. Recommendations from instructors or others qualified to evaluate academic ability.
