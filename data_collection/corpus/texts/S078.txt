International Students can now proceed to apply for the 2024 Binghamton University Scholarship to pursue an undergraduate degree in the United States. Binghamton University is providing the selected international students with $30,000 per year to support their study costs in form a full tuition fee waiver. Unlike some institutions, Binghamtom University is a world-class academic institution. It offers a wide range of interdisciplinary training, and does so with an international perspective. Binghamton University Scholarship Summary Host Country: USA Study Abroad: Study in USA Category: Undergraduate Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | $30,000 per year. Binghamton University Scholarship Details: Applications for the Binghamton University Scholarship 2024 have commenced. All students are considered for scholarships at the Binghamton University. Most of the scholarships are in form of stipends. In some cases too, they receive tuition scholarships. The vision of Binghamton is to design a distinctly global educational experience and foster an inclusive campus community that infuses all aspects of college life with an international perspective. Binghamton University Scholarship Requirements: To be considered for the Binghamton University Scholarship 2024, you must: have undergone the admissions process. follow the applications instructions for undergraduate international students at Binghamton University. submit scores of the following English language proficiency tests: TOEFL – 80 to 95 IELTS – 6.5 to 7.0 Duolingo – 110 to 120 Binghamton University Scholarship Benefits and Duration Twenty incoming first-year students receive a scholarship of $10,000 per year for up to four years of study, so long as the scholarship recipient maintains a specified GPA. Five incoming first-year students will receive a scholarship up to the in-state full cost of attendance (approximately $30,000) per year for up to four years of undergraduate study, so long as the scholarship recipient maintains a specified GPA. Required Documents for Binghamton University Scholarship To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; Official transcripts Language proficiency proof Financial documents like International Student Financial Statement.
