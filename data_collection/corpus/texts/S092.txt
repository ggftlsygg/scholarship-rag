For the academic year 2023/24, students who complete their initial registration to pursue their studies and register to study at a Recognised Teaching Centre in Pakistan or India will be eligible for a 25% discount on the Initial Registration Fee for the following programmes: CertHE in Business Administration and BSc Business Administration. BSc Business Administration: The discounted Initial Registration fee 2023/24 will be £1380 (£1840 less 25%/£460)CertHE Business Administration: The discounted Initial Registration fee 2023/24 will be £688.50 (£918 less 25%/£229.50). If you are eligible, you will be provided with a discount code that can be used when you register.
