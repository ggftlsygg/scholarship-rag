COMPUTER SCIENCE: FULLY FUNDED EPSRC DTP PHD SCHOLARSHIP: REAL-TIME SPOKEN LANGUAGE UNDERSTANDING FOR SOCIAL CARE ROBOTS 
https://www.swansea.ac.uk/postgraduate/scholarships/research/computer-science-epsrc-dtp-su-phd-real-2024-rs607.php

Key Information Funding providers: Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council (EPSRC) Doctoral Training Partnerships (DTP) and Swansea University's Faculty of Science and Engineering Subject areas: Intelligent Robotics and Natural Language Understanding. Eligibility Candidates must hold an undergraduate degree at 2.1 level in Computer Science; Mathematics; Linguistics or Psychology/Cognitive Science (with evidence of programming and/or NLP/computational linguistics experience), or an appropriate master’s degree with a minimum overall grade at ‘Merit’ (or Non-UK equivalent as defined by Swansea University). Evidence of publishable standard projects is also desirable. If you are eligible to apply for the scholarship but do not hold a UK degree, you can check our comparison entry requirements (see country specific qualifications). Please note that you may need to provide evidence of your English Language proficiency. Due to funding restrictions, this scholarship is open to applicants eligible to pay tuition fees at the UK rate only, as defined by UKCISA regulations. If you have any questions regarding your academic or fee eligibility based on the above, please email pgrscholarships@swansea.ac.uk with the web-link to the scholarship(s) you are interested in. Funding This scholarship covers the full cost of UK tuition fees and an annual stipend of £19,237. Additional research expenses will also be available.
