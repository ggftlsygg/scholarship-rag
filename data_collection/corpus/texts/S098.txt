Canon Foundation Research Fellowship 
https://www.canonfoundation.org/programmes/research-fellowships/

Programme DescriptionAnnually, the Canon Foundation in Europe grants up to 15 fellowships to European researchers to enable them to continue their studies in Japan. The same applies to PhD researchers based in Japan in relation to their studies in Europe. This offers the scholarship holders the unique opportunity to exchange skills and knowledge while at the same time promoting cultural and scientific understanding between the two regions. The research grants cover a wide range of topics - from molecular biology and garden architecture to automotive studies and genealogy. Target GroupEuropean and Japanese researchersAcademic RequirementsApplicants should have obtained at least a Master's or PhD degree within the last 10 years of applying to the Canon Foundation. Exceptions are possible. Number of Scholarshipsup to 15 annuallyDurationminimum period of 3 months up to maximum of 1 yearScholarship ValueCanon Fellows from Europe are free to choose their host institutes and hosts in Japan. The same freedom is given to Japanese Canon Fellows coming to Europe. The financial support for Research Fellows may amount up to 30,000 EUR per year and pro-rata for different periods.
