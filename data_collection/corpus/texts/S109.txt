Details and information about the scholarship Countries where the study will take place: Germany Scholarship name: DAAD Scholarship Place of study: One of the German public universities and participation in the DAAD scholarship Degrees you can obtain: All applicants can obtain a master's and doctorate Age: There is no specific age to apply for this scholarship Language Certificate: An English language proficiency certificate is not a mandatory requirement to apply for this scholarship Deadline to apply for the scholarship: The deadline for applying for the DAAD scholarship varies depending on the program you wish to study. Scholarship and funding features The DAAD scholarship to study in Germany offers free scholarships every year to students wishing to study a master’s and doctorate. There are different advantages it offers, and you can view all the advantages below: Depending on the level of study you are studying, a masters student receives a monthly stipend of 900 euros or 1,300 euros for doctoral candidates. Payments for health, accident and personal liability insurance coverage Travel allowance, unless these expenses are covered by the home country or another funding source Under certain circumstances, scholarship holders may receive the following additional benefits: Monthly rental support A monthly allowance to accompany family members. Documents required to apply for this scholarship There are a number of documents that must be attached to apply for this scholarship, and these documents must be translated into English. Below you can view these documents: Fill out the application for the grant from here CV (the CV must be in European style and signed by hand) A personally signed letter of motivation (indicating current profession and choice of graduate program(s), maximum 2 pages) with current date. Important: When applying for more than one postgraduate course (max. 3 courses), you must submit one letter of motivation explaining why you are applying for these specific courses and why you have chosen this priority. A letter of recommendation from your current employer; The letter must contain an official signature and stamp and must be of current date (not in a sealed envelope) Employment certificate(s) from the employer(s) proving at least two years of relevant work experience (beyond a bachelor’s degree) in Time of application and, if applicable, guarantee of re-employment from your current employer upon return home. Proof Language skills: English – IELTS or TOEFL (if you want to study in English) German – required for courses taught in German (if you want to study in German) Copies of academic transcripts Copies of academic transcripts, incl. Grading scale (certified translation if necessary) Applicants from the People's Republic of China are required to submit an APS certificate with their application documents. Language requirements For German language courses (the scholarship includes a 6-month German language preparatory course in Germany): Successful passing of the DSH 2 or TestDaF 4 language exam before the start of the course. Therefore, the minimum German language level of B1 at the time of application is a requirement that needs to be proven by submitting a current certificate. Note: The required German language courses (DSH or TestDaF) cannot be passed without any prior basic knowledge of the German language (at least B1 level), even with the six-month intensive course in Germany. Passing a language test is an essential prerequisite for enrolling at the relevant German university. For English language courses: TOEFL or IELTS certificate (minimum score: 550 on paper, 213 on computer, 80 on Internet)
