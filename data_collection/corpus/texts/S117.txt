The David Oyedepo Foundation Scholarship will grant students the opportunity to study at Covenant and Landmark University, Nigeria. Covenant University is the leading universities in Africa equipped with world-class facilities. It drives a vision to raise a new generation of leaders and reinstate the black race’s dignity. David Oyedepo Foundation Scholarship Summary Host Country: Nigeria Study Abroad: Study in Africa Category: Undergraduate Scholarships Eligible Countries: African Countries Reward: Full Scholarship. David Oyedepo Foundation Scholarship Details: The David Oyedepo Foundation scholarship programme is the major platform for promoting the Foundation’s vision and goals. The scholarship program awards full-ride scholarships to qualified individuals who are indigenes and residents of African countries. David Oyedepo Foundation Scholarship Requirements: To be considered for the David Oyedepo Foundation Scholarship, you must: Be a citizen and permanent residents of any African Nation Be between the ages of 16 and 25 at the time of application submission. Be eligible to receive a Nigerian student visa Be proficient in reading, writing, and speaking English Have a record of exceptional performance at the secondary school level which is benchmarked by: At least 50 points above JAMB cut-off points (for Nigerians only) 80% recorded average or 4.0GPA/5.0 in Secondary/High School Transcript Already have begun admission applications to Covenant or Landmark University. David Oyedepo Foundation Scholarship Duration and Reward Full Academic fees for a maximum of 5 years. (In accordance with the University stipulated time for the duration of the chosen course).
