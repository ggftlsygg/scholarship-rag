The Deakin University HDR Scholarships for international students are now open for interested and qualified students willing to pursue a postgraduate research and PhD degree in Australia. For all selected applicants, the Deakin University HDR Scholarships offer fully-funded awards of full tuition waiver, annual stipends of $28,900, a relocation allowance of $1500, and comprehensive health insurance. Deakin University HDR Scholarship Details Deakin university offers a range of government and University-funded scholarships available to support you during your candidature. The RTP scholarships are funded by the Australian Government, while the DUPR scholarships are funded by Deakin University. Both awards are available to domestic and international students. PhD and research degree scholarships can cover tuition fees, overseas student health cover, living allowances paid as stipends, and relocation allowances. Deakin University HDR Scholarship Requirements To be considered for the Deakin University Scholarship, you must: be a domestic or international student enrolled in an HDR course of study at Deakin University completed at least four years of tertiary education studies not be receiving an equivalent award or scholarship from the Commonwealth designed to offset HDR fees not be receiving income from another source to support that student’s general living costs while undertaking their course of study if that income is greater than 75 percent of that student’s RTP or DUPRS Stipend rate. have achieved 80% or equivalent in the research component of their previous study or an equivalent level of academic attainment, taking into account previous study, relevant work experience, research publications and other research experience meet all other minimum entry requirements according to the Higher Degrees by Research (HDR) Admission, Selection and Enrolment Procedure. Required Documents To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; CV Academic transcripts Valid English test result/evidence of Language of instruction Bachelor, Honours or Masters thesis/dissertation. Publication A statement of the research project/problem/question.
