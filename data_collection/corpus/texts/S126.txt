The Opportunity Funds Program annually provides financial support that covers the costs of application, GRE and TOEFL exams, as well as costs of visa and travel expenses. About the Education USA Opportunity Funds Program The Education USA Opportunity Funds Program (OFP) is an annual study-in-USA opportunity funded by the US Embassy in Nigeria for Nigerian students. OFP’s mission is to assist talented and determined, low-income students who are good candidates for financial assistance from U.S. colleges and universities but lack the financial resources to cover the up-front cost of obtaining admission. Since 2004, the EducationUSA Opportunity Funds Scholarship Program has placed 400 students at the most selective colleges and universities in North America with full aid. To be eligible for the Education USA Opportunity Funds Program, candidates must posses; High strong academic records Excellent participation in extracurricular activities Involvement in leadership role Outstanding community service participation.
