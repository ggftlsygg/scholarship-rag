European Union CSAS Scholarship (Intra-Africa Mobility Scheme)
https://c-sas.org/index.html

The scholarship cofounded by the African and the European Unions offers selected African students full scholarships that cover the costs of tuition, accommodation, medical insurance, travel, and a living allowance of €28,440. European Union CSAS Scholarship Details: The Intra-Africa Academic Mobility Scheme of the European Union has provided funding to support training of graduate students in Central and East African universities. In this mobility programme five African Partner Institutions and one EU Technical Partner will collaborate in the training of professionals in developing climate smart agrifood systems. The European Union CSAS Scholarship shall equip students and staff with a comprehensive understanding of climate-smart agrifood systems, incorporating disciplines such as agriculture, environmental science, climate science and socio-economic aspects of food systems. To be considered for the European Union CSAS Scholarship, you must: Be an African applicant Must be living in Africa as at the time of your application Applicants are only allowed to apply to study in an institution outside of their home country
