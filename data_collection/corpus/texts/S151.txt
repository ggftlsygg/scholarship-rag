Generation Google Scholarship
https://buildyourfuture.withgoogle.com/scholarships/generation-google-scholarship/#!?detail-content-tabby_activeEl=high-school-seniors

Be a current high school senior Intend to be enrolled as a full-time student in a 4-year BA/BS program in the US (excluding territories) or Canada in Fall Intend to be enrolled at a university in a computer science, computer engineering, software engineering or related area department Be able to demonstrate an interest in and motivation for studying computer science. The program is open to qualified students who meet the above criteria and we strongly encourage students from historically underrepresented groups, including Black/African American, Hispanic/Latino, American Indian, or Filipino/Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander, to apply. The Award Amount$10,000
