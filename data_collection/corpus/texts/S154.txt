German Bundestag Scholarship is an opportunity for politically and socially committed young graduates from Southern, East, and West Africa. If awarded the scholarship, you will get €700 stipend, free accommodation, travel expenses coverage, and health, accident, and liability insurance. German Bundestag Scholarship Summary Host Country: Germany Study Abroad: Study in Europe Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: Africa Reward: Full Scholarship + €700 stipend + Accommodation + Travel expenses. German Bundestag Scholarship Scholarship Details: The German Bundestag Scholarship is aimed at training African students who want to take an active and responsible role in shaping the democratic future of their countries. Since 2020, the German Bundestag has been offering a scholarship program for talented and politically interested young university graduates from sub-Saharan Africa. The scholarship considers candidates who are interested in the German parliamentary system and are actively committed to basic democratic values ​​in their home countries. To be considered for the German Bundestag Scholarship, you must meet the requirements below: Citizenship: From Botswana, Namibia, South Africa (Southern Africa); Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda (East Africa); Ghana, Nigeria, Senegal (West Africa) Age: Below 30 at the start of the scholarship on January 1st Education: Completed university studies Language: Proficient in German (at least B2 level) strong interest in political contexts as well as social/political commitment. Benefits of 2025 German Bundestag Scholarship Scholarship Value: €700, free accommodation, travel expenses coverage, and health, accident, and liability insurance Internship: One-week placement in a German Bundestag member’s office Curriculum: In-depth knowledge of parliamentary democracy, political decision-making, and workshops on societal issues Required Documents for German Bundestag Scholarship To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; Application Form Passport photo Letter of Recommendation Letter of motivation in German with your handwritten signature (maximum two pages); Copy of passport or copy ID; Degree certificate from the university Proof of German Language proficiency.
