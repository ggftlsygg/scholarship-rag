Every year, 70 fellowships worth $70,000/Year are offered to applicants who are Canadians, permanent residents, or international students. Banting Fellowship Program Summary Host Country: Canada Study Abroad: Study in Canada Category: Fellowships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: $70,000/Year No IELTS Required. anada Banting Fellowship Program Details: The Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships program provides funding to the very best postdoctoral applicants, both nationally and internationally, who will positively contribute to the country’s economic, social, and research-based growth. The objective of the Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships program is to: attract and retain top-tier postdoctoral talent, both nationally and internationally develop their leadership potential position them for success as research leaders of tomorrow. Eligibility Requirements for Canada Banting Fellowship Program To be considered for the Canada Banting Fellowship Program, you must: Be a domestic, permanent resident, or an international applicant fulfill or have fulfilled all degree requirements for a PhD, PhD-equivalent or health professional degree: between September 15, 2021 and September 30, 2025 (inclusively), and before the start date of their award. Applicants who have not fulfilled all requirements for their degree at the time of application must submit proof no later than October 15, 2025. For applicants who have completed more than one PhD, PhD-equivalent or health professional degree, the eligibility window applies to the most recent of these degrees. Benefits of Canada Banting Fellowship Program The Banting Postdoctoral Fellowships program provides funding worth $70,000 per year for a maximum 2 years.
