Colombia Foreign Scholarship Summary Host Country: Colombia Study Abroad: Study in USA Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships | PhD Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | $250,000 Stipends | Accommodation | Travel Costs No IELTS Required Deadline: June 9, Colombia Foreign Scholarship Details: Applications are now open for the / Government of Colombia Scholarship. This program is aimed at foreigners who wish to pursue their postgraduate studies in Colombia, as well as for foreigners from non-Spanish-speaking countries who need to strengthen the Spanish language to carry out postgraduate studies in Colombia. The Government of Colombia scholarships offer two types of applications: For foreigners who wish to pursue postgraduate studies in specialization, master’s, and doctorate degrees in public and private Colombian higher education institutions. For foreigners from non-Spanish speaking countries who wish to carry out postgraduate studies in specialization, master’s, and doctorate degrees in public and private Colombian higher education institutions. Eligibility Requirements for Colombia Foreign Scholarship To be considered for the Government of Colombia Scholarship, you must: be a foreign citizen Not have Colombian nationality. Not be residing, nor have resided the last 6 months prior to applying for the scholarship in Colombian territory or have started studies in Colombia at the time of application to the corresponding call. In case of being selected, scholars can be asked by ICETEX to provide the Certificate of Migratory Movements, with a date no greater than 30 days at the time of entering Colombian territory. Must not have any marital or de facto relationship in Colombia. Not having been a beneficiary of ICETEX through the Beca Colombia program. g) Not be older than 50 years. Have a cumulative general average of 4.0 out of 5.0 in undergraduate to apply the call. According to the grading scale of each country, the note must be equivalent to a minimum of 4.0 for the Colombian scale. Have a professional, undergraduate, or graduate degree in any area of knowledge. Have admission to at least between one and three postgraduate programs found in the Academic Offer Catalog -2 annexed to this announcement. Have good physical and mental health, certified by a doctor. Benefits of Colombia Foreign Scholarship The Government of Colombia Foreign Scholarship offers;the coverage of the entire tuition will be granted by the Colombian higher education institution Medical and hospital assistance a letter for the online application of the courtesy visa ICETEX will support each foreign scholarship holder with a monthly support (living expenses) each scholarship holder will also receive an amount for installation expenses in Colombia ICETEX will also support each scholarship holder with a value for books and materials Recipients will also receive a financial support equivalent to COP$250,000 to cover costs related to the issuance of the Foreign Identification. Required Documents for Colombia Foreign Scholarship To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; Letter of pre- admission or final admission from the university Letter of Academic Recommendation Certificate of undergraduate or graduate transcripts Personal Statement (Essay) Health and Medical certificate Professional experience certificates CV or Resume Copy of valid passport
