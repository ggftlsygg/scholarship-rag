Application for the fully funded Imperial College London DeepMind Scholarship has been announced for all interested and qualified students to study in the United Kingdom. The DeepMind Scholarships are offered to both home and international students to cover the full tuition fees, and provide a stipend of £25,320 to cater for living, equipment, and travel costs. In this post, we will provide you with the detailed information of the benefits, requirements, and procedures that will help you navigate the step-by-step application process for the DeepMind Scholarship without any difficulty. Imperial College London DeepMind Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Masters Scholarships | PhD Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | £25,320 Stipends | Travel Costs Deadline: May 17, Imperial College London DeepMind Scholarship Details: The DeepMind Scholarships seek to fund MSc and PhD degrees for students from groups under-represented in computer science and AI. The scholarships are aimed at encouraging and supporting wider participation, particularly from women and those from black and minority ethnic backgrounds. For October entry, there are two DeepMind MSc scholarships available, to students with a confirmed place on the MSc in Artificial Intelligence, the MSc in Computing (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning), or the MRes AI and Machine Learning. Requirements for Imperial College London DeepMind Scholarship To be considered for the DeepMind Scholarship, you must: Be a woman or Be from an underrepresented minority ethnic group Hold an offer for a postgraduate study in MSc in Computing (Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning), or the MRes AI and Machine Learning. Apply for admission before May 17th, Benefits of Imperial College London DeepMind Scholarship The scholarships cover: – full payment of tuition fees, – a stipend for living expenses of £21,420, – an equipment grant of £1,700, – a travel grant of £2,200 for attendance at conferences.
