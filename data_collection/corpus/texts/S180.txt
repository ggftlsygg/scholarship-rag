Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship Program

Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship offers scholarships to international teachers who wish to conduct research on school education at designated Japanese universities. As Teacher Training Students under the Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship Program, foreign students who study in higher education institutions are selected on the recommendation of Japanese Embassy/Consulate General, University, or Authority. Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship Summary Host: Japanse Governement Study Abroad: Study in Asia Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships | Training Eligible Countries: All Countries. Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship Details: The Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship allows a number of applicants to be nominated by the Graduate School of Science to the University of Tokyo. The number to be subsequently nominated by the University of Tokyo to MEXT, shall be finalized after the application period has closed. Its aim is to foster human resources who will become bridges of friendship between the grantee’s country and Japan through study in Japan and who will contribute to the development of both countries and the wider world. To be considered for the Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship, you must:ationality: Applicants must have the nationality of a country that has diplomatic relations with Japan. An applicant who has Japanese nationality at the time of application is not eligible. Age: In principle, applicants must be born on or after April 2, 1989. Academic and Career Background: Applicants must be graduates of universities or teacher training schools and have worked as teachers at primary/secondary educational institutions or teacher training schools. Japanese Language Ability: Applicants must be willing to learn Japanese. Applicants must be interested in Japan and be willing to deepen their understanding of Japan after arriving in Japan. Health: Applicants must submit a health certificate in the prescribed format signed by a physician. Visa Requirement: An applicant shall, in principle, newly obtain a “Student” visa at the Japanese diplomatic mission located in the applicant’s country of nationalit. Required Documents for Japanese Government MEXT Scholarship To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; Application Form Placement Preference Application Form Certified grade transcript for each academic year from the last university/ teacher training school attended The diploma from the last university/ teacher training school attended Certificate of employment Recommendation letter from the immediate supervisor at work Medical certificate
