rospective Master’s and PhD students should apply for the Kent State University Scholarship for an opportunity to study in the USA. The Kent State University Graduate Scholarship offers a full tuition waiver and monthly stipends in the form of assistantships and allows students to focus exclusively on their studies and research. Kent State University Scholarship Summary Host Country: USA Study Abroad: Study in USA Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships | PhD Scholarships | Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | Monthly Stipends No IELTS Required. Kent State University Scholarship Details: The Kent State University Graduate Scholarships are offered as graduate assistantships to international students pursuing graduate Master’s or PhD degrees at the university. Many graduate programs offer fellowships or one of three types of graduate assistantships: administrative, teaching or research. Students are encouraged. To be considered for the 2024 Kent State University Graduate Scholarship, you must: Be applying to pursue a graduate degree at KSU Meet the eligibility criteria for your chosen program Meet the English Language proficiency requirements of KSU
