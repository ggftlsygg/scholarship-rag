International Student Scholarships The LSA Scholarships Office awards a small number of International Student Scholarships each year to first-year incoming LSA students and international transfer students. These awards will be renewable for two or four years of study (depending on your admittance status) and recipients are selected based on information provided through their scholarship application and their undergraduate admissions application. What does the scholarship cover? For qualified LSA students, the International Student Scholarship helps to cover the cost of attendance. Amounts and Duration Award amounts can go up to 25,000 USD. All International Student Scholarships are renewable for two or four years (depending on admittance status) of undergraduate study, provided students maintain eligibility by completing their requirements each academic year. Eligibility Requirements To qualify for an International Student Scholarship, a student: Must be an incoming first-year or transfer undergraduate student admitted to a degree program in the College of LSA. Students will be considered even if they have not yet enrolled. Must NOT be a U.S. Citizen or Permanent Resident. Must be enrolled (or intend to enroll) full-time (12 credit hours or more) each term.
