UK and EU residents can receive a 20 per cent fee bursary for the Global MBA.When you receive your offer for the Global MBA, you should contact us(Opens in new window) to request the bursary. The bursary does not cover module continuation fees, recognition of prior learning fees, or any fees charged by examination centres or teaching institutions. If you are eligible, you will be provided with a discount code that can be used when you register. To apply or for more information, contact us.
