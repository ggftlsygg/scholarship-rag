Madonna university Assistance Award
https://www.madonna.edu/tuition-and-financial-aid/scholarships-and-grants/

Madonna University is committed to supporting students in pursuit of their goals through a range of scholarships and grants awarded based on academic performance, financial need, athletic experience, and group affiliation. Scholarships and grants are awards that do not need to be repaid and are awarded at the time of admission to Madonna. Need-based academic award for students who exhibit financial need as determined by the FAFSA. Award only applies to students at the Livonia Campus living on campus. Eligibility Must be enrolled full-time. Must be pursuing a first degree (not a second bachelor’s degree). Benefits The benefits and monetary value of the scholarship are unknown.
