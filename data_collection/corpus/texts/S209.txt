With a practical methodology, the MSc Sustainable Financial Management with Data Analytics will give you the chance to work on practical projects with real companies that will allow you to experiment with what you have learned in class. After graduating, you’ll have the keys to transform companies into positive contributors to the world. Moreover, you will count on leading practitioners and internationally recognized academics, who will lead you through a full immersion in sustainable finance This sustainable financial management with data analytics program will allow you to get hands-on practice during an optional 4-to-6-month internship in a top national or international company. Additionally, you will benefit from a professional mentoring program that will set you on your way to finding your ideal professional path. The MSc Sustainable Financial Management with Data Analytics is targeted at junior professionals who are passionate about sustainability – and looking to launch their careers in finance, accounting, or control departments. The program is grounded in a global perspective with a sustainable approach, while considering the added value that data analysis brings to the management of any company. Based in the heart of Barcelona, one of the top 10 cities in the world in which to invest, this course will equip you with the competencies needed to analyze complex financial problems with cutting-edge data-analysis techniques and technologies. In addition, the program will allow you to assume leadership in the paradigm shift towards sustainability that companies are requiring at this time, including a module focused on obtaining the Certificate in ESG Investing (provided by the CFA Institute). The program will train you to make managerial decisions, from a plurality of perspectives in a complex, cross-border, and transforming socio-economic world. If you decide to study this MSc your upcoming professional career will exist at the intersection of finance and sustainability.
