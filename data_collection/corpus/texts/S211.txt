Fully funded Memorial University Masters Scholarships are currently available at the Faculty of Medicine for international students willing to study in the Canadian-based university. The Memorial University Masters Scholarship is offering to cater for the full financial fees related to the study of the selected applicants- tuition, living stipends, and health insurance. Memorial University Masters Scholarship Summary Host Country: Canada Study Abroad: Study in Canada Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship + Stipends No IELTS Required. Memorial University Masters Scholarship Details: The Faculty of Medicine at the Memorial University of Newfoundland, in collaboration with the Marathon of Hope (MoH), is offering a unique opportunity for a motivated student to pursue a Master’s in Bioinformatics Research. The Memorial University Masters Scholarship program is specially designed for those who wish to advance cancer research, with a particular focus on colon cancer. Eligibility Requirements for Memorial University Masters Scholarship To be eligible for the Memorial University Masters Scholarship, applicants must satisfy the following conditions: Bachelor’s degree in a relevant field (computer science, bioinformatics, molecular biology). Strong motivation to learn sequencing data analysis and develop cancer research skills. Ability to work independently and as part of a team. Excellent written and oral communication skills in English. Benefits of Memorial University Masters Scholarship 2024 Financial support for the duration of the Master’s program. Opportunity to work on cutting-edge research projects sponsored by the Marathon of Hope. Access to state-of-the-art equipment and technologies for sequencing data analysis. Supervision by renowned researchers in the field of bioinformatics and cancer research. Required Documents for Memorial University Masters Scholarship To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; a detailed CV, a motivation letter explaining their interest in the position and how they meet the eligibility criteria, along with the contact information of two academic or professional references.
