Miami University Presidential Scholarship provides robust, immersive, and transformative learning experiences to international students willing to study in the United States of America (USA). The Miami University Presidential Fellows Program offers a four-year, renewable scholarship covering tuition, fees, housing, and food. Additionally, all fellows receive a stipend $5,000 as academic enrichment . In this post, we will highlight the details, requirements, benefits, and the step-by-step process required for a successful application to the Miami University Presidential Scholarship. Miami University Presidential Scholarship Summary Host Country: United States of America (USA) Study Abroad: Study in USA Category: Undergraduate Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | Accommodation | $5,000 Stipend. Miami University Presidential Scholarship Details Miami University is pleased to offer the Presidential Fellows Program (PFP), an elite scholarship opportunity that provides the brightest and best scholars and leaders of the 21st century with an unprecedented and exceptional college experience. Miami University makes college experience invigorating and rewarding by providing a nationally recognized support system anchored by professors dedicated to you and your future. The Miami University Presidential Scholarship Program package will cover tuition and fees for eight fall/spring semesters provided that you maintain a 3.50 cumulative grade point average each year. Benefits of Miami University Presidential Scholarship Once you are selected for the Miami University Presidential Scholarship, you will receive the following benefits; A four-year, renewable scholarship covering tuition, fees, housing, and food. A one-time $5,000 academic enrichment stipend. Tailored programming for the Presidential Fellows cohort, including Miami’s U-Lead Conference. Required Documents for Miami University Presidential Scholarship To apply for the Miami University Presidential Fellows Program, you must submit the following documents; Academic Transcripts Letters of Recommendation SAT/ACT Score Personal Statement National ID/Passport
