The Monash University Graduate Scholarship annually offers eligible domestic and international students willing to study in Australia, fully funded scholarships with stipends. The Monash University Scholarships are offered to eligible students to cover full tuition fees and a stipend of $$35,013 per year. In addition, the scholarship also open up opportunities for you to connect with alumni and industry partners. Monash University Graduate Scholarship Details Monash University are committed to training the next generation of research leaders and have over 1,000 graduate research students engaged in our areas of expertise. Monash University is ranked #40 in Best Global Universities and her scholarship has given immense support financially, especially to international students. They are able to participate in extra-curricular activities, complete an internship, and focus on research projects, instead of having to work part-time. PhD and Masters by Research scholarship opportunities are currently available with leading engineering researchers who are involved in pioneering research across 32different research themes. Monash University Graduate Scholarship Requirements To be considered for the Monash University Graduate Scholarship, you must: Hold a bachelors degree Have graduated from a well ranked university Have authored peer-reviewed research publications Possess excellent written and verbal English skills meet the Monash University’s requirements.
