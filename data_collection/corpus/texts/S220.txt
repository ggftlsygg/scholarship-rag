About the Monash University Scholarship The Australian Government offers Research Training Program (RTP) Scholarships to support domestic and international students undertaking Research Doctorates and Research Masters degrees. As part of this programme, an RTP Stipend (living allowance) is available to help support domestic and international students with living costs while studying at Monash. Details and information about the scholarship The country where the study will take place: Australia Donor: Monash University University where you will study: Monash University Academic degrees that you can obtain: You can obtain a master’s or doctoral degree The scholarship is offered to all students from all over the world. Study programs available There are many different programs and specializations that you can study through this scholarship, as this scholarship includes all study programs. You can learn about all study programs below: the sciences Design and architecture Humanities and social sciences Education Engineering Business Management Medicine the law information technology Health Sciences the pharmacy. Documents required to apply for the grant To apply for the scholarship program, applicants must submit their application online and upload the following list of documents before the application deadline. You can find out the required documents below: Fill out the university application passport copy Graduation certificate and transcript of the last academic qualification you obtained the biography Writing articles within the application 2 letters of recommendation English language proficiency certificate.
