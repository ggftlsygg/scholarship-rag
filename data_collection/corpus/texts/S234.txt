Newcastle University Business School is offering several Newcastle University MBA Scholarships to eligible students from within and outside the United Kingdom. The Newcastle University MBA Scholarships are awarded as full fees of £31,000 payable towards the cost of tuition fees for the entire duration of the Master’s degree program. In this post, we will provide the details, requirements, benefits, and the step-by-step application procedure for the Newcastle University MBA Scholarship. Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | £31,000 Deadline: April 12, Newcastle University MBA Scholarship Details: As a Business School we contribute, Newcastle University is committed to shaping an inclusive and sustainable future for business and society. The Newcastle University MBA Scholarship rewards candidates who can demonstrate substantial experience of leading sustainable and or social business initiatives. The successful candidate will also be an inspirational role model for peers and colleagues looking to become sustainable business leaders of the future. Submissions need to be in English. have worked or currently work in a sector which is moving to more sustainable business models e.g. energy, packaging.
