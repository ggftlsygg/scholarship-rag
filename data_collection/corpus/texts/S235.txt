North American University Scholarship
https://www.na.edu/admissions/undergraduate/scholarships/

Applications for the North American University Scholarships are currently ongoing for international and domestic students willing to study in the United States of America. The North America University Foundation Scholarships award qualified international students $22,000 every year to pursue undergraduate degrees of their choice in the university. North American University Scholarship Summary Host Country: USA Study Abroad: Study in USA Category: Undergraduate Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries. No IELTS Required. North American University Scholarship Details The North America University Office of Career & Success Services is committed to giving undergraduates, graduate students, and alumni the tools and resources they need to transition from school to a successful career. Institutional scholarships are available to qualified undergraduate freshman and transfer students. Scholarship recipients must meet all University requirements for regular admission in the fall and spring semester for eligibility. The North American University, USA is offering an award amount of $22,000 to students who need support in covering their study expenses in the USA. Requirements for North American University Scholarship To be considered for the North America University Scholarship, you must: Meet all University requirements for regular admission in the fall and spring semester Be a fresh international or domestic applicant Meet the English proficiency requirements Must have excellent academic records. Benefits of the North American University Scholarship The North America University Scholarship is worth between $12,000 to $22,000 per year based on the academic merit and SAT scores of eligible candidates.
