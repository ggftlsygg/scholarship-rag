The Northern Illinois University Scholarship offers graduate assistantships that covers the costs of your tuition, health insurance, and stipends that will help you cover living costs every year for the period of your graduate study. Northern Illinois University Scholarship Summary Host Country: USA Study Abroad: Study in USA Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships | PhD Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | Living Stipends No IELTS Required. Northern Illinois University Scholarship Details: The Northern Illionois University Scholarships are offered annually as graduate assistantships to international students pursuing Masters and PhD scholarships at the university. Graduate assistants are an integral part of the intellectual community at Northern Illinois University. The purpose of graduate education is to develop scholars and professionals equipped with the knowledge and skills to succeed in their chosen fields. By working under the supervision of faculty mentors or administrative professionals, graduate assistants gain valuable experience relevant to their profession. Students who hold assistantships experience educational and professional benefits. To be considered for the Northern Illinois University Scholarship, you must: Be applying for admission to study a Master’s or PhD degree at NIU. Meet the entry requirements for your chosen program Meet the English Language requirements of the university Must demonstrate competence in spoken English to be appointed as a graduate teaching assistant engaged in oral classroom instruction.
