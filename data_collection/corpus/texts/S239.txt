Onsi Sawiris scholarship to study in the United States of America
http://www.onsisawirisscholarship.org/undergraduate-scholarship/

Onsi Sawiris Scholarship is a special merit-based scholarship program established in 2000 by Orascom Construction as part of the company's commitment to promoting excellence and achievement. Orascom Construction has launched the Onsi Sawiris Program to invest company resources in educational programs to improve the communities in which it operates. This program is a precedent in Egypt for the private sector to participate in educational programs on a sustainable basis. The aim is to facilitate the academic development and character building of Egyptian youth and give them the necessary tools to develop their careers and thus benefit the communities in which they live. Since 2000, the Onsi Sawiris Scholarship Program has funded the educational dreams of 93 outstanding students. Through the Onsi Sawiris Scholarship Program, Orascom Construction offers full scholarships to Egyptian students seeking to pursue their degrees at prestigious universities in the United States with the aim of enhancing Egypts economic competitiveness. Onsi Sawiris Scholarships are awarded on the basis of merit, need, and character as demonstrated by academic excellence, extracurricular activities, and entrepreneurial initiative. Scholarships include full tuition fees, living allowance, travel and health insurance. Details and information about the scholarship The country to be studied in: The United States of America The donor: ONSI SAWIRIS Foundation The place of study: One of the public universities in the United States of America The academic degrees you can obtain: All applicants can obtain a bachelor’s and master’s degree The scholarship is provided to Egyptian students for a period Study for Bachelors: 4-year Bachelors degree program Duration of study for Masters: 2-year Masters degree program. Benefits of the grant and funding The Onsi Sawiris Scholarship offers many, many free scholarships every year in order to support and encourage students who wish to study in America and travel to the United States of America. The scholarship covers all round-trip travel costs The scholarship covers all study costs The scholarship provides a monthly stipend The scholarship provides free accommodation and health insurance. Universities available for those wishing to study bachelor's degrees The Onsi Sawiris Scholarship Program is awarded to selected students to pursue a bachelor’s degree at one of the following US universities: University of Chicago Stanford University University of Pennsylvania Harvard university Massachusetts Institute of Technology. Fields of study for those wishing to study a bachelor’s degree engineering Economics finance administration. Admission requirements for those wishing to study a bachelor’s degree Undergraduate applicants applying for the Onsi Sawiris Scholarship Program must meet the following minimum requirements: They must be Egyptian citizens residing in Egypt (preference will not be given to dual nationality applicants). GPA of 3.5/90% in high school or equivalent high school diplomas (Nb) Students who have completed their grade 11 by the application deadline (31 July 2023) and students who have completed their grade 12 by the application deadline (31 July 2023) are both eligible to apply for the university scholarship program). TOEFL ® as proof of English language proficiency: Minimum score required is 100 (obtained within two years). Minimum SAT I score: 1450 (obtained within 2 years) or minimum ACT score: 32 (obtained within 2 years). Extracurricular Activities: You must have participated or are currently participating in extracurricular activities. Are planning to begin their studies in the United States in 2024. Have not lived more than two years abroad. Preference is given to applicants who do not reside outside Egypt. You must commit to returning to Egypt for two years immediately after the successful completion of your bachelor’s degree.
