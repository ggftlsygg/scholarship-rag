Pearl Lemon Scholarship
https://pearllemon.com/pearl-lemon-scholarship/

Pearl Lemon is an award-winning SEO and digital marketing agency. With their official head office in London, they’re all about diversity and flexibility – which is why their tight-knit global workforce operates on a 100% remote basis! Bootstrapped by their founder and CEO Deepak Shukla, they offer the most substantial and innovative SEO available, with over 228 positive reviews from professionals and businesses in all fields. Eligibility You’ll need to be enrolled, or soon-to-be-enrolled, as a student at an accredited college or university (this includes distance learning). You must have a good grasp of the English language. You must have a desire to learn new digital marketing skills. Benefits £1,000 cash award for the best essay. 3-5 hours of free mentorship from their founder. Application They’ll need your relevant personal details, such as the name and address of your university, the course you’re enrolled in, where they can reach you, etc. They’d also like you to submit a 600-1000 word essay answering the following question: How will your personal values determine your education goals, as well as your goals for the future? Applicants will only be contacted when it is relevant to their application. Applicant information can be disposed of upon their request. All information (proof of enrollment, essays, and contact details) are strictly confidential and will only be shared with relevant parties. Applicants will not be asked to provide any sensitive or personal information (except the details found in the application form) at any point in time. Applicants retain all rights to their essay. All submitted essays and videos MUST be your original work.
