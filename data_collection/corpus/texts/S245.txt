The prestigious fully-funded Peter Carpenter scholarship is awarded to African citizens with top level academic qualifications. The Peter Carpenter Africa Scholarship is offered under the very generous support from Sussex alum Peter Carpenter, to African students to undertake PhD projects on Africa climate science. In this post, we will discuss the details, requirements, benefits, and the step-by-step procedure for the Peter Carpenter Scholarship. Peter Carpenter Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | PhD Scholarships Eligible Countries: African Students Reward: Full Scholarship No IELTS Required Deadline: April 15, Peter Carpenter Scholarship Details: The Peter Carpenter Africa Scholarship programme is hosted within the Climate Science and Society Research (CSSR) group in the Department of Geography, where there is a vibrant research programme into the African climate and related fields. The program’s aims are to better understand the nature, causes, impacts and consequences of climate variability/change and their representation in models, leading to improved climate information to support climate resilience. The PhD project can focus on any aspect of African climate science, to be decided taking account of candidates’ interests, experience and career goals. Requirements for Peter Carpenter Africa Scholarship To be considered for the Peter Carpenter Africa Scholarship, you must: Hold a a first-class degree in natural sciences Meet the entry requirements for a PhD degree at the University of Sussex Meet the English Language requirements of the University of SussexBenefits of Peter Carpenter Scholarship The Peter Carpenter Africa Scholarship is fully funded. All expenses including tuition, accommodation, living expenses at UKRI rates are paid for by the Peter Carpenter Africa Climate Scholarship
