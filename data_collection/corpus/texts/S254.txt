The Qingdao University Scholarship funded by the Shandong Provincial Government is designed for international students willing to pursue undergraduate, Master’s, or Doctoral programs in China. Qingdao University Scholarship Summary Host Country: China Study Abroad: Study in Africa Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships | PhD Scholarships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | Accommodation | Medical Insurance. Qingdao University Scholarship Details: The Qingdao University Scholarship is funded by the Shandong Provincial Government for international students to pursue undergraduate and postgraduate programs in Qingdao, China. Since 1987, Qingdao University has been a hub for international students, providing quality education and comprehensive support. Recognized as a key institution in China, the university offers a broad spectrum of accredited programs ranging from Bachelor’s to Doctorate degrees. To be considered for the Qingdao University Scholarship, you must: be a non-Chinese citizen in good health. apply for admission to the university before the deadline meet the English Language requirements of the university For undergraduate programs, applicants must have a high school diploma. For Master’s programs, a Bachelor’s degree is required. Doctoral program applicants must hold a Master’s degree. Benefits of Qingdao University Scholarship 2024 This Qingdao University Scholarship ensures that students can focus on their studies without the burden of financial constraints, making it an excellent opportunity for those aspiring to study abroad. It covers the full tuition fee, on-campus accommodation, and comprehensive medical insurance. Required Documents for Qingdao University Scholarship 2024 To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; Application Form Diploma from College (notarized copy). College transcript. Copy of passport. Updated resume detailing personal and family background, along with academic and professional experiences. English proficiency certificate (if applicable). Two recommendation letters from associate professors or scholars. All documents must be in English or Chinese and should be original or notarized copies.
