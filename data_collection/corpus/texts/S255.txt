The Kings College London is inviting applications for the The Raheem Sterling Foundation Scholarship is worth up to £16,250 per year and will be awarded as an annual fee waiver for the amount of £9,250 per student and a stipend for the amount of £7,000 per student. Raheem Sterling Scholarships for undergraduate studies. Raheem Sterling Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Undergraduate Scholarships Eligible Countries: Black African or Caribbean Reward: Full Scholarship | £16,250 Stipends No IELTS Required. Raheem Sterling Scholarship Details: The Raheem Sterling Scholarships are open to Black or Caribbean students from economically disadvantaged backgrounds from the Greater London area. It is worth up to £16,250 per year. The Raheem Sterling Foundation envisions a world where young people will always have the support they need in the pursuit of improving their opportunities, their future careers and their quality of life. Through his charitable organisation, Raheem Sterling is dedicated to helping those from disadvantaged backgrounds break down barriers to success, find their voice, and realise their true potential in the world. To be considered for the Raheem Sterling Foundation Scholarship, you must: Identify as belonging to one of the following ethnic groups: Black or Black British – Caribbean Black or Black British – African Mixed – white and Black Caribbean Mixed – white and Black African Other Black background Have firmly accepted an offer to study for an undergraduate full time degree programme at King’s College London starting in September 2024. Have been assessed for fee status as a ‘home’ student. Have a home postcode from the Greater London area as stated in their UCAS application. Benefits of Raheem Sterling Scholarship 2024 The Raheem Sterling Scholarship will cover the course tuition fees and a stipend. The award will be paid in the form of an annual fee waiver for the amount of £9,250 per student and a stipend for the amount of £7,000 per student. The award is for a maximum of three years. Students will not receive funding for repeat years, a year abroad or interruptions.
