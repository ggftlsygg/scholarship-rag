Royal Veterinary College Bursary
https://www.london.ac.uk/study/fees-funding/funding-study/scholarships-bursaries/royal-veterinary-college-bursary

Royal Veterinary College graduates who wish to take the Veterinary Epidemiology and Public Health programme (Pg Cert, Pg Dip and MSc) are entitled to a 20 per cent bursary. Further informationWho can applyThe bursary is available to the following Royal Veterinary College students: Those who have already completed a 240-hour individual module and now want to progress to the degree programmeRVC graduates who now wish to register for the Veterinary Epidemiology and Public Health programme (Pg Cert, Pg Dip and MSc)How to applyIf you wish to apply for the bursary you will need to provide appropriate documentation showing your RVC alumni status. You can select this bursary in the ‘Scholarships and bursaries’ section of the online application form. OrIf you wish to apply for the bursary after you have submitted your application for the programme you can do so via our online enquiry system(link to enquiry form). Please ensure to provide your student number. Application deadlineYou must submit your request for this bursary either on your application form or via the online enquiry system at least 2 weeks before the registration deadline for your programme.
