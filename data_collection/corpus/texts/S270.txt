Samsung American Legion Scholarship
https://www.legion.org/scholarships/samsung

Scholarship applications are restricted to high school juniors who attend the current session of either The American Legion Boys State or American Legion Auxiliary Girls State program and are a direct descendant - i.e. child, grandchild, great grandchild, etc. - or a legally adopted child of a wartime U.S. military veteran who served on active duty during at least one of the periods of war officially designated as eligibility dates for American Legion membership. World War I - Apr. 6, 1917 - Nov. 11, 1918 World War II - Dec. 7, 1941 - Dec. 31, 1946 Korean War - June 25, 1950 - Jan. 31, 1955 Vietnam War - Feb. 28, 1961 - May 7, 1975 Lebanon/Grenada - Aug. 24, 1982 - Jul. 31, 1984 Panama - Dec. 20, 1989 - Jan. 31, 1990 Persian Gulf War - Aug. 2, 1990 - Until cessation of hostilities Includes Operations Enduring Freedom, Iraqi Freedom, & War on Terrorism Applicants who are direct descendants of Korean War Era Veterans will receive 3 bonus points. Applicants with membership to the American Legion Auxiliary or Sons of the American Legion will receive a bonus point. Or, they can receive a bonus point by uploading a membership card of a parent. No more than one bonus point for an application. The Award AmountUp to $10,000
