Spring Hill College is a private Jesuit liberal arts college located in Mobile, Alabama, USA. The college offers various scholarship programs to its students. Here are some of the programs and their criteria: 1. Academic Scholarships Presidential Scholarship: $24,000 - $28,000 per year Criteria: 3.5 GPA, 28 ACT or 1300 SAT Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must maintain 3.0 GPA Dean's Scholarship: $20,000 - $22,000 per year Criteria: 3.2 GPA, 24 ACT or 1140 SAT Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must maintain 2.75 GPA Faculty Scholarship: $16,000 - $18,000 per year Criteria: 2.8 GPA, 20 ACT or 1020 SAT Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must maintain 2.5 GPA 2. Talent-Based Scholarships Fine Arts Scholarship: $2,000 - $4,000 per year Criteria: Audition or portfolio review in music, theatre, or visual arts Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must participate in fine arts programs Athletic Scholarship: Varies Criteria: Athletic ability and coaches' recommendations Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must participate in intercollegiate athletics 3. Need-Based Scholarships Spring Hill College Grant: Varies Criteria: Demonstrated financial need Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must maintain Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) Federal Pell Grant: Varies Criteria: Demonstrated financial need, FAFSA completion Conditions: Renewable for up to 6 years, must maintain SAP 4. Other Scholarships Jesuit High School Scholarship: $2,000 per year Criteria: Graduate of a Jesuit high school Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must maintain 2.5 GPA Legacy Scholarship: $1,000 per year Criteria: Parent or grandparent attended Spring Hill College Conditions: Renewable for up to 4 years, must maintain 2.5 GPA Transfer Scholarship: $2,000 - $4,000 per year Criteria: Transfer student with 3.0 GPA or higher Conditions: Renewable for up to 2 years, must maintain 2.75 GPA General Conditions and Criteria All scholarships are subject to change and may be adjusted based on institutional or federal funding. Students must maintain full-time enrollment (12 hours) to receive scholarships. Scholarships are renewable for up to 4 years (or 2 years for transfer students) as long as the student meets the required GPA and other conditions. Students must complete the FAFSA to be considered for need-based aid. International students may be eligible for scholarships, but must meet additional requirements
