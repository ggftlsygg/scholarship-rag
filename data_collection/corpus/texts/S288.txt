Tennessee Concrete Association High School Essay Contest
https://form.jotform.com/92387134178161

Open to all high school students ages 16-18 at the beginning of the school term. Essay must be typed, double-spaced and not exceed 600 words. Complete this online form to submit essay. Essays will be judged on content and writing ability (grammar, style, clarity, etc.). Remember, this is an essay . Essays should demonstrate a student's awareness of concrete's role as a building material in homes, commercial buildings, streets and highways (consider what was left standing after Hurricane Michael).
