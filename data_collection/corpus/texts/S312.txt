UEA Across the Pond Award
https://www.uea.ac.uk/study/fees-and-funding/scholarships/norway-lanekassen-award

this scholarship is​ an automatic award for students who are a national of Norway and aims to reduce the overall cost of overseas tuition. Eligibility UEA are delighted to link up with Across The Pond to offer this prestigious award to Norwegian students commencing a course at UEA in September 2023. The award will not be applied for year in industry or study abroad years. Benefits The award is an automatic award applied at point of offer and is worth £4,000 per year of full time study at UEA for a maximum of three years. Submissions are judged on a monthly basis and successful candidates will have their scholarship applied to three years of full time study, whilst excellent submissions will be considered for £10,000 per year.
