UNESCO Poland Fellowship Program
https://www.unesco.org/en/fellowships/poland-engineering?hub=986

The program is a 6-month internship program that offers selected beneficiaries living expenses, accommodation, free tuition, and access to the university facilities. UNESCO Poland Fellowship Summary Host Country: Poland Study Abroad: Study in Europe Category: Training | Fellowships Eligible Countries: All Countries Reward: Full Sponsorship | Accommodation | $1,100 Monthly Allowance | Travel Expenses. Applications are invited for 30 UNESCO POLAND Co-Sponsored Research Fellowships for applicants from Africa, Arab states, Asia and the Pacific, Europe, Latin America, and the Caribbean. UNESCO is the United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization. It seeks to build peace through international cooperation in Education, the Sciences and Culture. UNESCO’s programmes contribute to the achievement of the Sustainable Development Goals defined in Agenda 2030, adopted by the UN General Assembly in 2015. UNESCO Poland Fellowship Requirements: To be considered for the UNESCO Poland Internship 2024, you must satisfy the following requirements: Applicants must hold the Bachelor’s or M.Sc. degrees. Applicants from outside the home country will often need to meet specific English language/other language requirements in order to be able to study there. Candidates should have a Bachelor of Eng (B.Eng) or a Master of Engineering (M.Eng) degree, Candidates applying for Archeology Fellowship must hold a B.A. or M.A. degree, preferably in the area of Archaeology or Conservation; Be proficient in reading and writing in English; No more than 40 years old.
