This scholarship is known as the University Scholarship Program provided by the United States Agency for International Development 2024-2025. This program is implemented by the American University in Cairo. Advertisements This program offers scholarships to all third-year secondary school students who wish to study for a bachelor’s degree, meaning to join the university next September 2025. This scholarship provides fully-funded scholarships to study for a period of four or five years, and the number of years depends, of course, on the specialization the student chooses to study. This program allows students to study in one of the best Egyptian universities such as the American University in Cairo, Zewail City for Science and Technology, Badr University, Assiut University, Cairo University, Ain Shams University, Alexandria University, or Mansoura University. Students accepted into the university scholarship program provided by the United States Agency for International Development 2024-2025. Accepted students are allowed to study in the English language as well as study on the credit hour system. USAID Scholarship Program 2025. The scholarship provides training courses to learn and develop the English language skills necessary for study as well as for the labor market in Egypt This scholarship gives you the opportunity to study at one of the universities in the United States of America for a full semester and also completely free of charge, as they pay all costs such as travel tickets, visas, university costs, and living costs, as well as a monthly stipend to support living in the United States of America, for the purpose of working on personal and professional development. In the following you can learn about all the mandatory and optional documents to apply for this scholarship: A personal photo (mandatory requirement) A copy of the national ID card (mandatory requirement) A copy of the national ID card of the guardian - father, mother, or legal guardian (mandatory requirement) Proof of official monthly electricity consumption (mandatory requirement) A statement of all the family’s bank or postal accounts for the last 6 months, whether savings, salary, current, postal, loans, or other accounts (if any) A letter from the employer of the student’s guardian confirming the salary and incentives (salary items), pension, or a copy of the tax certificate for the past year in the case of self-employment (mandatory requirement) If the family is a beneficiary of the “Solidarity and Dignity” program of the Ministry of Social Solidarity, it must attach its card or a copy of a certificate or a stamped government document proving this (mandatory requirement) A certificate stamped with the Eagle seal proving that the school and all academic years in the secondary stage in which the student is registered are a government or experimental school or the School of Excellence for Science and Technology (STEM) (mandatory requirement) Evidence that the student has obtained the awards and certificates mentioned in the form (if any) Photocopies of rental contracts, home ownership contracts, or family property (mandatory requirement) Additional documents required from students with disabilities (certificates proving disability) (mandatory requirement) What are the conditions for applying for this scholarship? In order to be eligible to apply for this scholarship, you must meet some conditions, and these conditions are not complicated, and you can view them below: The student must be of Egyptian nationality The student must be enrolled in one of the government schools, the Egyptian experimental school, or the STEM schools throughout the years of secondary school Obtaining a high school graduation certificate in the academic year 2021-2022, with a total of no less than 85% of the first round. The student must be a graduate of science or mathematics only Only students with disabilities are accepted in the Literary Division Submit all required documents proving the financial condition of the family Active participation in extracurricular/extracurricular activities such as community development and public service Having a personality with leadership qualities For people with disabilities: minimum knowledge of assistive technology for learning and submitting an educational and psychological evaluation document Important notes that you should pay attention to The deadline to apply for the scholarship is May 31, 2024 Students are accepted from the Science Division and the Math Science Division Students with disabilities are accepted from the literary section The language of study in all departments will be English and the credit hours system You must be aware that students are distributed into different specializations according to the university coordination standards and the places available at each university.
