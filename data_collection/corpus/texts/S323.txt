Scholarship Benefits You will receive these benefits if you get University of Helsinki Scholarship 2025 in Finland An award that is fully funded (tuition fee + 10 000 euros) Fees for the full academic year A wide range of healthcare services will be provided for free or at a very low cost Discounts on Unicafe meals for students Public transport reductions A low-cost Unisport service. Eligibility Criteria Applicants from all over the world are eligible to apply for the scholarship A bachelor’s degree is required for applicants to apply for a Master’s Scholarship. The scholarship application is the same as the application for admission. No separate application is needed. Instructors and others do not need to be contacted. Stipends are automatically paid upon acceptance. Required Documents Bachelor’s degree final transcript. Updated CV. Personal statement of 300 to 350 words. Pdf Scanned passport.
