The University of Edinburgh Glenmore Scholarship are available for eligible postgraduate programmes offered by the University in the - academic session. The scholarship awarded for selected online master’s programs at the university covers the full tuition fee coverage for the entire duration of the scholarship. This post contains the full details of the scholarship. We will explain the eligibility requirements, benefits, duration, and the complete application procedure for interested applicants. University of Edinburgh Glenmore Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: Developing Countries Reward: Full Scholarship No IELTS Required Deadline: May 30, Eligibility Requirements for University of Edinburgh Online Scholarship To be considered for the University of Edinburgh Online Scholarship, you must: Be a citizen from a developing country Be accepted into one of the eligible Human Medical postgraduate programmes offered by the University in the - academic session. Be committed to continue to work in countries that will benefit most from their furthered medical education. Priority will be given to those who have not had the opportunity to study at Masters level previously. Benefits of University of Edinburgh Glenmore Scholarship The scholarship will cover full tuition fees for the duration of the programme of study. For onsite programmes, duration is one year For online programmes, duration is 2 or 3 years
