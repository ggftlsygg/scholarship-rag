The University of Edinburgh Surgery Online Scholarship is currently open for qualified students interested in studying at the UK-based world-class university. Each scholarship will cover full tuition fees, subject to satisfactory progress, with consideration being given to a contribution towards the cost of internet access, printing, and project costs. This post contains a breakdown of the summarized details, eligibility criteria, benefits, duration, deadline, and step-by-step application procedure for eligible candidates. Edinburgh Surgery Online Scholarship Details: The University of Edinburgh will offer a number of Surgery Online Scholarships for online learning programmes to interested individuals who are seeking to pursue a Master’s program at the institution. The surgery award is an online full tuition fee scholarship with considerations of internet access, printing and project costs. The University of Edinburgh is a prestigious UK university. The latest international university rankings have reaffirmed Edinburgh’s position as a leading global institution. The QS World University Rankings have placed the University 20th in the world. Edinburgh Surgery Online Scholarship Requirements: To be considered for the Edinburgh Surgery Online Scholarship, you must meet the following requirements: be a national and resident in an eligible country, please click HERE for the full list. have at least a conditional offer for Master’s at Edinburgh be residents in Africa, South Asia, Caribbean Islands, Pacific Islands or Central and South America. Benefits of Edinburgh Surgery Online Scholarship Each scholarship will cover full tuition fees, subject to satisfactory progress, with consideration being given to a contribution towards cost of internet access, printing and projext costs. Required Documents for Edinburgh Surgery Online Scholarship To apply, candidates must be ready to submit the following documents; Basic Medical Degree (MBChB or equivalent), as recognised by the General Medical Council. Final Degree Transcript for your basic medical degree. This is an official University document, normally though not always issued at the same time as the degree is awarded. It lists all the courses and grades you received during your degree. Details of one referee. You will be given the opportunity to enter your referee’s email address on the online form; the system will then email your referee automatically. A personal statement confirming you are continuing your learning in a supervised clinical environment. Evidence of English language. If you are not from an English speaking country (as defined by UK Visas & Immigration), you will need to provide evidence of your proficiency, such as an IELTS or TOFEL certificate. Eligible Programmes for Edinburgh Surgery Online Scholarship The scholarships will be awarded to applicants who are accepted for admission on to the; MSc Surgical Sciences (Online Learning) and ChM (Online Learning) General Surgery, Urology, Trauma & Orthopaedics, Vascular & Endovascular.
