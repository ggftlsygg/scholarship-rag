The University of Manchester Raheem Sterling Scholarship is offered in partnership with the University of Manchester, Kings College London, and the Raheem Sterling Foundation. The scholarship will have your tuition fees paid for all three or four years of your undergraduate study while also providing an annual bursary of £8000. University of Manchester Raheem Sterling Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Undergraduate Scholarships Eligible Countries: Black African or Caribbean Reward: Full Scholarship | £8000 Stipends/Yr No IELTS Required. University of Manchester Raheem Sterling Scholarship Details: The University of Manchester and Kings College London are proud to partner with The Raheem Sterling Foundation to offer scholarships to support Black African and Caribbean heritage applicants from socio-economically underrepresented backgrounds. The Raheem Sterling Foundation envisions a world where young people will always have the support they need in the pursuit of improving their opportunities, future careers and quality of life. Through his charitable organisation, Raheem Sterling is dedicated to helping those from disadvantaged backgrounds break down barriers to success, find their voice, and realise their true potential in the world. The Foundation will fund seven students on undergraduate degree courses at The University of Manchester over the next three years. Have made an application to Student Finance England (SFE) Benefits of University of Manchester Raheem Sterling Scholarship For 2024 entry, the scholarship is available to students studying three or four year degrees. Successful applicants of the award will:have your tuition fees for paid for all three or four years of your undergraduate study; receive an annual bursary of £8000 for all three or four years of your undergraduate study. Awards will be paid in instalments from Semester 1 for theacademic year if you’ve met the terms of your course offer and completed registration with the University.
