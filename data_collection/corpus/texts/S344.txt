the University of South Australia Scholarship is currently open for interested and qualified international students willing to study and conduct research in Australia. The scholarship award is open to domestic and international students with full funding including a tuition waiver, $32,500 annual stipends plus other benefits. To be considered for the 2024 University of South Australia Scholarship, you must; Be an international student Be applying for a Masters research degree or PhD program Apply for a project from a list of available research projects Meet the eligibility requirements for the project you are applying for.
