Tasmania Graduate Research Scholarship Details Tasmania Graduate Research Scholarships are available across all research disciplines at the University of Tasmania and will be awarded to applicants with demonstrated research ability. Scholarships are allocated competitively based on academic merit, experience, prior learning and other relevant attainments. Priority is given to new candidates in strategic research areas. Seize the golden opportunity and submit your application for the University of Tasmania Graduate Research Scholarship before the deadline. To be eligible for a University of Tasmania Graduate Research Scholarships: Successful applicants must have completed at least four years of tertiary education studies and have achieved at least an upper second class Honours degree or equivalent. Awards are only available to those who will be undertaking a full-time research Masters or Doctoral degree in 2024. Candidates must not be receiving another equivalent award, scholarship or salary to undertake the proposed program (if that income is greater than seventy-five per cent of the base Research Training Program stipend rate). Required Documents for Tasmania Graduate Research Scholarship To apply for the Tasmania Graduate Research Scholarship, you will be required to submit the following documents; A copy of your passport A signed copy of the Supervisory support form A Research Proposal Evidence of your qualifications Certified copies of transcripts/academic records and testamur/degree certificates for ALL previous university studies, complete and incomplete. A copy of your research thesis (from your Honours or Masters degree) A Curriculum Vitae (CV), this must include the contact details of two referees Evidence of English language proficiency
