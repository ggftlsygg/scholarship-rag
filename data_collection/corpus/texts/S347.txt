Located on the west coast of Western Australia, the University of Western Australia was established in 1911 as a public research university. It is the 2nd ranked university in Australia in terms of undergraduate degree programs. It has a reputation for being one of the best universities in the country. Scholarship coverage: Fully Funded. Eligible nationality: All Nationalities. List of Available Study Fields: For Undergraduate Degree: There are no restrictions on which undergraduate degree program students can apply to except for the following: Direct Pathway to Juris Doctor Direct Pathway to Medicine Direct Pathway to Pharmacy Direct Pathway to Podiatry Direct Pathway to Dental Medicine. Eligibility Criteria: When applying for the University of Western Australia Scholarships 2025 in Australia, applicants must meet the following criteria: An applicant must be a citizen of one of the above-mentioned countries. A letter of offer from the university for a Bachelor’s or postgraduate degree is required. The following must be achieved: ATAR between 85.00 and 98.00; or A WAM between 70.00 and 85.00
