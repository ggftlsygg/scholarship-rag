The Visionary Women Scholarship recognizes female students who have an outstanding drive, talent, and vision and are characterized by their ambition, potential, and diversity of thought. They are not afraid to challenge the status quo and bring a unique perspective to the table. Eligibility Motivation Letter: ~450 words on their perspective of what defines a visionary leader and/or what changes are needed to increase the representation of women in the business community. Criteria: Need-based, female, motivation letter
