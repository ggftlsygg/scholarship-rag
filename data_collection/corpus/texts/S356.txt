Applications for the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China scholarship are open now. International students from all around the world can apply for the Master’s and PhD degree programs at UESTC, China. There is no need for an IELTS test. It is a fully funded scholarship. Details Country: China Organization: UESTC Degree: you can obtain MS or PhD Who can apply? Students of all nationalities Available programs Business Administration Instrumentation Science and Technology Chemical Engineering and Technology Control Science and Engineering Public Administration Applied Economics Physics Politics Foreign Language and Literature Biomedical Engineering Mechanical Engineering Material Science and Engineering Electrical Engineering Information and Communication Engineering Computer Science and Engineering Electronic Science and Technology Optical Engineering Software Engineering Management Science and Engineering Benefits provided by the scholarship This scholarship offers many different benefits, which you can learn about below: Full Tuition fees Free accommodation A monthly stipend of CNY 3,000 per month for Master’s A monthly stipend of CNY 3,500 per month for Doctoral students A Comprehensive medical insurance of CNY 800/person Documents required to apply for the Scholarship You must prepare the following documents to use in applying for this grant. The required documents are: Entry Application Form for International Students A copy of the previous stage education certificate A copy of the transcript Copy of the front page of the passport A Study Plan (undergraduate) / Research Plas 2 Recommendation letters Proof of no criminal record. Eligibility Criteria Applicants must be citizens of any country Except China, You must be in good health. Applicants should be under 35 years old apply for a Master’s degree and have a Bachelor’s degree. Applicants should be under 40 years old apply for a doctoral degree and have a Master’s degree. Applicants for Chinese medium programs must have at least the HSK Level Four Certificate (Test of Chinese Proficiency). Applicants for English medium programs require an English language test score report, 80 for TOEFL, and 5.5 for IELTS. you can also apply without IELTS/TOEFL if you have an English proficiency certificate.
