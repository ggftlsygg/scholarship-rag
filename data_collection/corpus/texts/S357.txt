The University of Michigan-Dearborn offers international undergraduate scholarships to students from all around the world. Students can apply for bachelor’s Degree Scholarships if they want to complete their undergraduate studies in the United States. Details Country: USA Organization: University of Michigan-Dearborn Degree: you can obtain BS Who can apply? Students of all nationalities. Available programs All of programs at the University Benefits provided by the scholarship This scholarship offers many different benefits, which you can learn about below: Scholarships worth $10,000 per year for up to four years will be offered through the University of Michigan Dearborn Undergraduate Award in the USA. Documents required to apply for the Scholarship You must prepare the following documents to use in applying for this grant. The required documents are: Entry Application Form for International Students A copy of the previous stage education certificate A copy of the transcript Copy of the front page of the passport 2 Recommendation letters Eligibility Criteria A grade point average of 3.0 (4.0 scale) A combined SAT score of 1020 or higher (ACT composite score of 20) Enrollment at the University of Michigan-Dearborn for full-time degree-seeking Renewable with a 3.0 GPA at UM-Dearborn A minimum score of 6.5 is required for IELTS, TOEFL (IBT-80, CBT-213, PBT-550), or MELAB (76).
