Applications are now closed for the 2026/7 Burnett Scholarship. Through the generosity of the Notsew Orm Sands Foundation, we have offered six scholarships in both 2024 and 2025, and will be doing so again this year for the 2026-27 cohort. The successful applicants receive a fully funded studentship on either of our taught Master's courses: MSc Conservation Science and International Wildlife Trade Applicants must meet the following criteria: - Be nationals of, or have official refugee status in, Angola, Botswana, Namibia, Zambia or Zimbabwe. - Have at least two years of work experience in the conservation sector. - Be in possession of a good undergraduate degree, with a minimum of a UK 2:1 or equivalent. - Meet the UK Visas and Immigration specifications and University of Kent English language requirements. Scholarship details The Scholarship covers full tuition fees, a monthly stipend and travel expenses to and from the UK. The scholarship is for the Taught Master’s course offered at DICE in Conservation Science. Deadline The deadline to apply was Sunday 1st March 2026. Applications will no longer be accepted.
