AFCEA War Veterans Scholarships $2,500 (https://www.afcea.org/war-veterans): The AFCEA Educational Foundation provides academic support to U.S. military personnel. Recipients must be currently enrolled and attending classes in an undergraduate program part time or full time in an eligible STEM major degree program at an accredited four-year college or university in the U.S. Deadline: May 1
