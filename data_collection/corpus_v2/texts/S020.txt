Further information Hide All Who can apply The Alumni Bursary is available to graduates of the University of London Worldwide programmes who are going on to further study with the University of London Worldwide. To be eligible for an Alumni Bursary, you must have successfully completed a bachelor's or master's degree with the University of London Worldwide through its distance or flexible learning programmes. How to apply To receive the alumni bursary: You apply for the next course or module that you wish to study. You can select the Alumni Bursary in the ‘Scholarships and Bursaries’ section of the online application form when you are applying for your programme. Your request for the Bursary will be processed once an offer of a place on the programme has been issued. Alternatively if you wish to apply for the Alumni Bursary after you have submitted your application for your programme, you can do so via our online enquiry system. Once you have received an offer of a place on the programme you can submit an enquiry and request the bursary. We verify your eligibility as an alumni of the University of London Worldwide (distance learning programmes) and issue you with a bursary voucher code. Please do not begin registration or make payment until you have received this code. By accepting the bursary, you understand and agree to the terms and conditions outlined in the section below. Application deadline Bursary requests are processed on a rolling basis. However we recommend that you submit a request for this bursary via the online enquiry system at least 2 weeks before the registration deadline for your programme. FAQs Who qualifies for the award of an Alumni Bursary? To be eligible for an Alumni Bursary, you must have successfully completed a bachelor's or master's degree with the University of London through its distance or flexible learning programmes (formally referred to as the International Programmes or External System) and you must be qualified as a graduate by the University. The programme previously undertaken must be a full degree. If you were awarded a Graduate Diploma or Certificate, this does not make you eligible for the Alumni Bursary. Which fees are eligible for a discount by applying the Alumni Bursary? For many courses, the Alumni Bursary consists of a 10% discount off a number of fees charged by the University of London. For the Global MBA course, the discount amount is 20%, and for other courses, the Alumni Bursary does not apply. LLB Alumni receive further discounts if they are applying for the LLM. Please see further details below. Fees qualifying for a discount are as follows: Registration and continuing registration fees, undergraduate exam entry fees, undergraduate examination resit fees, and module fees. For the BSc Computer Science course, the module continuation fees are covered by the Alumni Bursary. The discount does not apply to the following: application fees, recognition of prior learning fees, module continuation fees for postgraduate courses, registration extension fees, postgraduate examination resit fees, examination rechecks, and fees that are charged by third parties, such as local teaching support or for local exam centres. I am a graduate of the LLB and now wish to apply for the LLM – what discount do I receive? Alumni of the LLB receive a discount of 30% off the LLM fees. If the alumni is in Bangladesh, Pakistan or Malaysia, then they should use the LLM Bursary which allows them a 40% discount off LLM fees. Please note that UoL scholarships or bursaries cannot be combined with other UoL scholarships or bursaries, but can be combined with external bursaries, scholarships, or funding. Is the Alumni Bursary rate the same for the Global MBA programme? No, the Alumni Bursary for the Global MBA programme is 20%. Can the Alumni Bursary be applied retrospectively? No. The bursary is only valid for studies that begin, for Master’s and Bachelor’s degrees, in/after the 2016/17 academic year. For Certificates, Diplomas, and Individual Modules, they are only valid for studies that begin in/after the 2017/18 academic year. The Alumni Bursary cannot be retrospectively applied to studies before these periods. Please make sure that you receive your bursary code before making any payments. No refunds will be issued if you have a code and have not applied it or if you request a code after paying. I qualify for the Alumni Bursary but would like to pass it on to a family member. Is this possible? No, the Alumni Bursary can only be used by the relevant University of London graduate. Can my Alumni Bursary be used against fees charged by a Recognised Teaching Centre? No, the Alumni Bursary applies only to course-related fees charged directly by the University (see above). Can the Alumni Bursary be applied to full course fees as well as modules taken on a pay-as-you-go basis? Yes, certainly, the Alumni Bursary can be applied to both the full course fees and the pay-as-you-go model. Will I need a new bursary code each year? No, you will not need a new code each year. Your code will work for the duration of your course and is subject specific. If you move onto another course you will need to request a new bursary code. What must I provide to prove my eligibility? Your full name, student number, degree and year awarded will be sufficient for us to confirm your status. I obtained my degree on-campus at one of the University of London Federation Members. Do I qualify for this Alumni Bursary? No, this Alumni Bursary only applies to alumni who previously completed a degree with the University of London Worldwide through its distance or flexible learning programmes. Federation Member Alumni Bursaries are also available on some University of London Worldwide programmes if you have studied in person at one of the following University of London federation members: Goldsmiths, City, Queen Mary, Royal Holloway, SAS and ULIP. These are only available on certain programmes. Please see below for further details. I am an on-campus graduate of Queen Mary University of London. Would I qualify for the Alumni Bursary? You would qualify for the Federation Member Alumni Bursary for the Global MBA Course only. You would not qualify for the Alumni Bursary to study on any other courses provided by University of London. I am an on-campus graduate of City, University of London. Would I qualify for the Alumni Bursary? You would qualify for the Federation Member Alumni Bursary for the MSc Supply Chain Management and Global Logistics course only. You would not qualify for the Alumni Bursary to study on any of the other courses provided University of London. I am an on-campus graduate of Goldsmiths, University of London. Would I qualify for the Alumni Bursary? You would qualify for the Federation Member Alumni Bursary for the MSc Data Science course only. You would not qualify for the Alumni Bursary to study on any of the other courses provided by University of London. I am an on campus graduate of Royal Holloway, University of London. Would I qualify for the Alumni Bursary? You would qualify for the Federation Member Alumni Bursary for distance learning programmes where Royal Holloway is the academic lead institution only. You would not qualify for the Alumni Bursary to study on any of the other courses provided by University of London. I am an on campus graduate of School of Advanced Studies. Would I qualify for the Alumni Bursary? Yes you would qualify for the Alumni Bursary. I am an on campus graduate of University of London in Paris (ULIP). Would I qualify for the Alumni Bursary? Yes you would qualify for the Alumni Bursary. Can the Alumni Bursary be redeemed through on-campus programmes? No, the Alumni Bursary applies only to University of London degrees offered via distance learning. Can I use my Alumni Bursary against my Application Fee? No, the discount does not apply to the following: application fees, recognition of prior learning fees, module continuation fees for postgraduate courses, registration extension fees, postgraduate examination resit fees, examination rechecks, and fees that are charged by third parties, such as local teaching support or for local exam centres. I am a graduate from the External System – could I still qualify for the Alumni Bursary? Yes, we would need you to provide us with your full name, student number, degree awarded and the year of award but there should be no problem in your qualifying for the Alumni Bursary. I am a graduate from the International Programmes – could I still qualify for the Alumni Bursary? Yes, we would need you to provide us with your full name, student number, degree awarded and the year of award but there should be no problem in your qualifying for the Alumni Bursary. I am an LLB student who is re-attempting the LLB through the graduate entry route after successfully completing the LLB previously. Do I qualify for the Alumni Bursary? No, you cannot claim the Alumni Bursary for a degree which you have already completed. School of Advanced Study Alumni In addition to the University of London alumni bursary, those who hold a degree from the School of Advanced Study (SAS) or one of its institutes, are also eligible for a discount on future courses with SAS. If you progress to a further degree programme without a break, you will receive a 15% discount; otherwise, you will receive a 10% discount. For further information, please contact alumni@london.ac.uk University of London Institute in Paris Alumni In addition to the University of London alumni bursary, those who studied an undergraduate degree with the University of London Institute in Paris are also eligible for a discount on postgraduate study with ULIP. This is awarded via Queen Mary University of London, which is ULIP’s registering institution. For further information, please contact alumni@london.ac.uk
