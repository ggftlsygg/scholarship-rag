OSE places great emphasis on appreciating student contributions in extracurricular activities. Every semester, student achievements and contributions are recognized with scholarship awards. Applications for the Spring 2027 OSE Scholarships will open on October 5, 2026. ACTIVE STUDENT SCHOLARSHIP FOR SPRING 2027 The AUS Active Student Scholarship offered by the Office of Student Experience is aimed at providing scholarships to outstanding active students who demonstrate excellence in extracurricular activities and whose efforts yield exceptional results and inspire other students to become high achievers. - Criteria: - Undergraduate student with a minimum of 12 credit hours in the semester that the application is submitted - Minimum CGPA of 2.5 - Achievements in AUS extracurricular activities for Spring 2026 and Fall 2026. Those who have been actively involved in interest-oriented and cultural clubs, community service volunteers, leadership groups, Student Council, and academic associations and societies are encouraged to apply. - Good moral character with no disciplinary action on record. - Conditions: - The scholarship entitles the recipient to an award of up to 25 percent tuition remission for one semester. - Scholarship recipients must be registered for a minimum of 12 credit hours during the semester they are receiving the scholarship. - Application: - Click here to apply online. Applications are due by 9:00 a.m. on November 12, 2026. For more information, email [email protected]. ATHLETIC SCHOLARSHIP FOR SPRING 2027 The AUS Athletic Scholarship offered by the Office of Student Experience is awarded to talented student-athletes who excel in sports, exhibit good sportsmanship and dedication and establish a record of consistent participation. The athletic scholarship aims to encourage student participation and to develop and promote sports at the university. - Criteria: - Undergraduate student with a minimum of 12 credit hours in the semester that the application is submitted - Minimum CGPA of 2.5 - Remarkable achievements and record of active participation in AUS varsity teams for Spring 2026 and Fall 2026. - Good sportsmanship and good moral character with no disciplinary action on record - Conditions: - The scholarship entitles the recipient to an award of up to 25 percent tuition remission for one semester - Scholarship recipients must be registered for a minimum of 12 credit hours during the semester they are receiving the scholarship - Application: - Click here to apply online.
