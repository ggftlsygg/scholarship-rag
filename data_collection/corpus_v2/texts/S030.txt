Ampère Excellence Scholarships for International Students
https://www.scholars4dev.com/ampere-excellence-scholarships-for-international-students/

Brief description: The Ampère Scholarships of Excellence help excellent international students study for a Master’s at ENS de Lyon, one of France’s grandes écoles. About 20 scholarships are given each year. ENS de Lyon also offers GRADUATE+ scholarships of €1,000 a month for 10 months for the best international students on selected Master’s programmes in its graduate initiatives. Host Institution(s): ENS de Lyon in France Field(s) of study: All ENS de Lyon Master’s programmes open to international students in the exact sciences, the arts, and the human and social sciences (except the FEADéP Master’s programmes). Target group: International students from any country Scholarship value/duration: €1,000 a month for one year. The scholarship can be renewed for the second year if the head of the Master’s programme selects you and you pass the first year. It does not include a tuition waiver or housing. Eligibility: • for the first year of a Master’s (M1): a Bachelor’s degree (180 ECTS) or equivalent; • for the second year of a Master’s (M2): a first-year Master’s (240 ECTS) or equivalent. Application instructions: Update (13 November 2026): The 2027-28 application calendar has not been published yet. ENS de Lyon says it will be updated in November 2026. Last year, applications opened on 14 November 2025 and closed on 23 January 2026. One online form covers both the Master’s application and the scholarship application. There are separate forms depending on your profile, so make sure you use the right one. Do not apply through the Études en France platform. The 2027-28 dates have not been announced yet. Last year, the form opened in mid-November and closed in late January, with results in mid-March. It is important to visit the official website (link found below) for detailed information on how to apply for this scholarship.
