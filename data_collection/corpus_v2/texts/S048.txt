Overview Bond International Undergraduate Excellence Scholarships provide tuition remission scholarships to high academic achievers, applying to study a bachelor's degree. International Undergraduate Excellence Scholarships are awarded on the basis of academic merit, and are a testament to Bond University’s commitment to quality and outstanding international students. Being an International Excellence Scholar As an International Undergraduate Excellence Scholar, you will be awarded with 50% tuition remission for any single or approved combined undergraduate degree (excluding the Bond Medical Program), and will commence your studies in either the January, May or September semester.
