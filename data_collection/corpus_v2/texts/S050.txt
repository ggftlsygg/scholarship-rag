Need-Based Scholarships Boston University funds a number of scholarships for students who meet specific criteria for demonstrated financial need. To be considered, you must file both the Profile™ and FAFSA applications by the required deadlines. After the first year the BU Scholarship Assurance guarantees that your BU need-based scholarship aid will be renewed each year and recipients are not required to file the Profile™. The FAFSAThe Free Application for Federal Student Aid (FAFSA) is used to determine eligibility for state and federal grants, scholarships, and loans. The FAFSA can be completed on the Federal Student Aid website. The Boston University FAFSA school code is 002130. More is required each year to apply for federal and state financial aid. Will you qualify? Apply and find out! Trying to predict whether you’ll receive financial aid isn’t easy. You can only find out for sure by completing the applications, which we strongly encourage you to do. But, using the MyinTuition calculator may help you get a general idea of what’s possible. Charles River Housing Scholarship Awarded to students who meet all eligibility criteria for need-based financial aid and live in University-owned housing on either the Charles River Campus or the Fenway Campus. Students must have a housing charge to be eligible. Students receiving Resident Assistantships that cover their full housing and dining are not eligible to receive the Charles River Housing Grant. University Scholarship University Scholars are selected from among those with exceptional financial need and outstanding academic achievement and promise. Total financial aid is offered up to the student’s full financial need, as determined by BU Financial Assistance. A special provision of the University Scholarship ensures that your total BU scholarships will increase at the same percentage rate as any future tuition increase. BU Special Assistance Scholarship One-time BU Appeal Scholarship in recognition of circumstances impacting a student’s plan to pay for BU. Not included in BU Scholarship Assurance and will not be renewed in subsequent academic years. Eligibility is evaluated through BU’s Appeal for Financial Aid Reconsideration process. International Student Need-Based Scholarships BU offers need-based scholarships to international students through select partnership programs. BU does not pay for visa fees, summer or winter housing cost, or any tax liability for international students receiving a BU scholarship.
