British Council Women In STEM Scholarships About the award Level: Masters Course: Any eligible 1-year masters programme (Full details below) Country/Region: Bangladesh, India, Nepal, Pakistan, Sri Lanka Value: The award covers full overseas tuition fees, a contribution towards monthly maintenance allowance travel, visa and other initial costs. No. of awards: 5 Deadline: 14 April 2026, 23:59 BST More information Please note that the submission deadline has been moved to 14 April 2026. To be considered for the scholarship, in addition to submitting your application to Queen Mary University of London, you must also complete and upload the British Council Women in STEM Application Form [DOC 41KB] In partnership with the British Council, Queen Mary University of London is delighted to offer five scholarships for Women in STEM for the 2026-27 academic year. Access to education is one contributing factor leading to the underrepresentation of women in STEM. Therefore, the objective of this programme is to help address this situation by providing opportunities for women from the countries listed below to continue with their studies and research in STEM subjects. Eligible Programmes Eligibility Criteria - You must identify as a woman. - You must be a passport holder and permanent resident of one of the following countries: India, Nepal, Bangladesh, Sri Lanka, Pakistan. - You must meet the entry requirements for the programme you have applied for, including academic equivalents and English language. The deadline for students to meet any conditions of their offer is 1 August 2026. - You must have completed all components of an undergraduate degree at the time of applying for the scholarship. - Your application must demonstrate a need for financial support. - You must not have previously studied at a degree level or higher in the UK or lived recently in the UK. - If your application is successful, you must agree to return to your country of citizenship for a minimum of two years after the scholarship award has ended. - You must upload your completed British Council’s application form via our online portal (see link below). Additionally, we are looking for applicants who: - Are active in the field with work experience or with a proven interest in the programme area you are applying for. - Are willing to demonstrate future contribution to capacity-building and socio-economic advancement through the benefits achieved after graduating from UK higher education and returning to their home country. - Can demonstrate a plan and passion to engage other women and girls in STEM from your home country. To be considered for the scholarship you must also complete and upload the following British Council Women in STEM Application Form [DOC 41KB] to the 'Experience' tab on the application portal before the deadline. Applicants who have not submitted this form will not be considered for the scholarship. Please consider that the 'Experience tab' may prompt you to upload a CV; however, you should upload your British Council Women in STEM application form in this section instead. If you are uploading the British Council Women in STEM Application Form after the application submission, please upload/attach this mandatory form using the ‘Contact Us’ tab on the application portal. Once you have submitted your application, you will receive an acknowledgement email with your applicant ID and login details for the applicant portal. You can use the applicant portal to track the status of your application and upload additional documents including the application form. The British Council will make the final decision on selected scholars. If you are awarded a scholarship, you agree to maintain contact with the British Council and act as an ambassador for the UK and engage with activities as part of a British Council Scholarships for Women in STEM alumnus during and after your study in the UK. Results are expected to be announced in mid-June. The British Council's full terms and conditions of the Women in STEM scholarships will be shared with the shortlisted candidates.
