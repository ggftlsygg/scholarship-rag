Business Alumni Association Graduate Scholarship in Business
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Business Alumni Association Graduate Scholarship in Business. Amount: CAD 2,000. Term: Fall. Study load: Full Time or Part Time. Application: Required. Eligibility: Awarded annually to a Canadian citizen or permanent resident with superior academic standing (GPA of 3.5 or greater on a course load of 30 credits) who is being admitted to the first year of a Master of Business Administration degree program in the Faculty of Business. Awarded on the basis of academic excellence; however, community involvement and participation in student association activities also may be considered. Part-time students are eligible for this award.
