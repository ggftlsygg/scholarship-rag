Are you eligible? MA Human Rights Scholarships for the MA Human Rights programmes are available for applicants in the following countries: • Belize • Botswana • Cameroon • Dominica • Eswatini • Fiji • Gabon • Ghana • Grenada • Guyana • Jamaica • Kenya • Kiribati • Lesotho • Malawi • Malaysia • Maldives • Mauritius • Mozambique • Namibia • Nauru • Papua New Guinea • Rwanda • Samoa • Saint Helena • Saint Lucia • Saint Vincent and the Grenadines • Samoa • Sierra Leone • Solomon Islands • South Africa • Sri Lanka • Tanzania • The Gambia • Togo • Tonga • Tuvalu • Uganda • Vanuatu • Zambia To qualify, you must meet the entry requirements by holding an undergraduate degree (e.g. bachelor) comparable to a UK upper second class honours degree, plus English language requirements. Individual candidates will be selected on the basis of financial need; outstanding academic merit or potential (demonstrated particularly by past achievements); and knowledge-transfer and development potential. Candidates with a track record of inclusive practice and experience in promoting the human rights of marginalised groups are encouraged to apply. See full requirements on Postgraduate study. How to apply Key dates and deadlines Deadline to submit scholarship applications to the Commonwealth Scholarship Commission: 31 March 2026, 16:00 BST Applicants are responsible for ensuring that their applications for admission to the programme are submitted sufficiently early and with all required supporting documents so that an offer can be made and their subsequent scholarship application entered before the deadline. We recommend that programme applications are submitted no later than 16 March to allow this to be processed in time. Selection process The Commonwealth Scholarship Commission will share applications with participating institutions after the deadline, who will then nominate their chosen candidates to receive a Commonwealth Distance Learning Scholarship in June 2026. The CSC then checks that the candidates nominated meet the CSC’s eligibility criteria and confirms if an award can be made in July. Universities inform successful candidates only if they have successfully been awarded a Commonwealth Distance Learning Scholarship in August 2026. Unfortunately if you do not hear directly from us by the end of August, your application will not have been successful. We will indicate here when all successful applicants have been notified. If you have a query about your scholarship application once it has been submitted to them, please contact the CSC. About the award The award is worth the full course fee for the distance-learning MA programme. The MA Human Rights is the only programme of it's type to be offered by distance learning. Academic direction is provided by the School of Advanced Study, University of London.
