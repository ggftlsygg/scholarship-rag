Konrad Zuse School relAI MSc Program 2026 in Germany: Scholarship Opportunity for Students in Reliable AI Applications are now open for the Konrad Zuse School of Excellence in Reliable AI (relAI) MSc Program 2026, an advanced academic opportunity for students seeking specialized training in trustworthy and reliable artificial intelligence. Hosted in partnership with Technical University of Munich and Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich, this prestigious program offers selected students cross-disciplinary AI training, professional development, and industry exposure, alongside scholarship support. Designed for future AI leaders, the relAI MSc program equips participants with expertise in the rapidly growing field of reliable AI, focusing on safety, security, privacy, and responsibility in AI systems. About the relAI MSc Program The relAI MSc is an enhancement to a regular master’s degree program at TUM or LMU Munich, offering a flexible and personalized academic pathway for students pursuing advanced education in artificial intelligence. Participants benefit from: - Specialized training in reliable and trustworthy AI - Scientific and technical coursework - Professional development programming - Industrial exposure and networking - Research integration with leading AI experts Scholarship Benefits Funded participants receive: - Scholarship of up to €992 per month - Additional travel grants, including home travel support - Access to research and academic resources - Enhanced mentorship and development opportunities Research Areas Applicants should have interest in reliable AI topics across one or more of the following research areas: - Mathematical and Algorithmic Foundations - Algorithmic Decision-Making - Medicine and Healthcare - Robotics and Interacting Systems - Learning and Instruction Eligibility Criteria Applicants are encouraged to apply if they have: - An excellent bachelor’s degree in: - Computer Science - Mathematics - Engineering - Natural Sciences - Data Science / Machine Learning / AI-related disciplines - Strong interest in reliable AI topics such as: - Safety - Security - Privacy - Responsible AI - Certified proficiency in English Application Deadline Submit your application by: 15 June 2026 (23:59 AOE) How to Apply For more Scholarship Opportunities, Visit OFY here.
