Current Scholarship Openings DAVID OYEDEPO FOUNDATION (DOF) scholarship programme awards full scholarships to qualified individuals who are indigenes and residents of African countries. The scholarship seeks to develop youth leaders who are ready to put their learned skills to work in leading organizations, communities and their local countries. The scholarship grants students the opportunity to study at select courses at Covenant and Landmark University, Nigeria. Completed applications must be submitted no later than June 27th, 2025.
