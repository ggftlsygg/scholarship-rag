Dhunna Family Personal Development Award
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Dhunna Family Personal Development Award. Amount: CAD 2,500. Term: Fall. Study load: Full Time. Application: Required. Eligibility: Awarded annually to a full-time student entering their second year of studies in the Alberta School of Business MBA Program. Recipients must have achieved a minimum GPA of 3.3 after completion of the first year of MBA studies. Selection based on involvement in promoting or representing the Alberta MBA Program through extra-curricular initiatives and personal growth as demonstrated in their application. Entering the scond year: fast-track: 15-18 credits Students are to submit their application to the Faculty of Business. A complete application package consists of a document highlighting previous work experience and/or community involvement, a two page summary discussing what qualities are important to be a successful leader, and examples of a personal or academic weakness, and discussing unexpected or important lesson(s) learned while in the first year of the MBA program.
