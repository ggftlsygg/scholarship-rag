Doreen M Lougheed (Bcom'49) MBA Scholarship
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Doreen M Lougheed (Bcom'49) MBA Scholarship. Amount: CAD 8,000. Term: Spring/Summer. Study load: Full Time. Eligibility: Awarded annually on the basis of academic achievement to an outstanding female student, who is Canadian citizen and an Alberta resident, registered full-time. Nominee must be in the first year of the MBA program, and must be academically outstanding. The scholarship is granted for two years subject to review after the first year.
