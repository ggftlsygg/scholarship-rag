Dr Richard (Rick) Hyndman MBA Entrance Scholarship in Energy, Environment and Public Policy
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Dr Richard (Rick) Hyndman MBA Entrance Scholarship in Energy, Environment and Public Policy. Amount: CAD 4,500. Term: Fall. Study load: Full Time or Part Time. Application: Required. Eligibility: Awarded annually to a student(s) entering their first year of full-time or part-time study in the MBA program at the Alberta School of Business. Applicants must have a demonstrated interest in public policy and experience working in the field of energy/environment. The recipient(s) will be selected on the basis of superior academic standing and a letter of application outlining career aspirations.
