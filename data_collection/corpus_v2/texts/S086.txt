EF Pearson Chemical/Business Graduate Scholarship
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

EF Pearson Chemical/Business Graduate Scholarship. Amount: CAD 3,300. Term: Fall. Study load: Full Time or Part Time. Application: Required. Eligibility: Program of Study: MBA, MBA/MEng, or a Master's degree program specializing in Engineering Management - Selection Criterion: Nominees must hold an undergraduate degree in engineering, preferably in chemical engineering - Selection Criterion: Selection will be based on academic achievement - Additional Information: The School of Business & Faculty of Engineering will each select nominee(s). The IF Morrison Selection Committee will forward the nominee(s) to the FGSR
