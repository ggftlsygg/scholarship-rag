Ernst Ludwig Ehrlich Studienwerk: Doctoral Scholarship
https://eles-studienwerk.de/bewerbung/promovierendenfoerderung/

We support professionally recognized Jewish doctoral candidates in all subject areas and all disciplines (except medicine). We acknowledge patrilineality. Non-Jewish doctoral candidates can apply for research projects in Germany that deal with Jewish topics. Admission to doctoral studies at a state or state-recognized university in Germany and – if German citizenship – in an EU member state or in Switzerland. In justified cases, doctoral studies can also be funded at a non-European university. Doctoral candidates without German citizenship who are pursuing a doctorate at a German university must reside in Germany for the duration of their funding. The doctoral scholarship supports young scientists. Applications for admission to doctoral scholarship should therefore be submitted in the initial phase of writing the dissertation. The last degree should not have been completed more than five years ago at the time of application. When selecting applications, we take into account special circumstances in the biography as well as periods of care for children or relatives. Doctoral scholarships are awarded for the standard funding period of three years. At the end of the doctoral studies, there is also the possibility of an extension of another six months. We expect German language skills at level B2.
