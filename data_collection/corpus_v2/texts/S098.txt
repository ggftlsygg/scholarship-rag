Evelyn Maier Graduate Award in Business
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Evelyn Maier Graduate Award in Business. Amount: CAD 1,800. Term: Spring/Summer. Study load: Full Time or Part Time. Application: Required. Eligibility: Awarded to a student who identifies as female that registered full- or part-time in any year of a Master of Business Administration degree in the Alberta School of Business. Preference is given to a student with a background in Nursing. Selection is based on extra-curricular volunteer and/or community activites that demonstate leadership traits and character, and academic achievement
