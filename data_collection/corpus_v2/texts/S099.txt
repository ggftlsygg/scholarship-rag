Doctoral scholarships / Application In line with its statutes, the Studienstiftung supports "the university education of young people who, because of their exceptional academic or artistic talents and personal qualities, can be expected to make an outstanding contribution to society as a whole". Its programmes include comprehensive support for doctoral students. Applications are invited from highly qualified and socially committed PhD students, in conjunction with their academic supervisors. Application requirements - The doctoral candidate has been accepted for a doctoral programme at a German university where the thesis will also be submitted; nationality is irrelevant. German citizens pursuing a Ph. D. abroad may also apply; in this instance, applicants must provide an explanation for their choice of university. Applications will be assessed subject to available funds. - At the time of application, the most recent degree must not be older than four years and three months. Periods of professional qualification, for example legal or teacher training (Referendariat) do not constitute grounds for an extension of the four-year deadline; pregnancy, childcare, periods of extended illness or inability to work as well as periods of care for dependents will be taken into account. Relevant documentation must be supplied. - The funding must start within the period specified in the award letter; otherwise, the offer will expire. - Applications may be submitted no earlier than the final semester of a master’s programme or an equivalent course of study. If a final degree certificate or diploma is not yet available at the time of application, applicants should submit a transcript of records of all completed degree courses. This will form the basis for the review procedure. Before the application can be submitted to the selection board for final consideration, a degree certificate or an official confirmation of successful completion and a full transcript of all courses must be provided. It is important that all applications include a well-developed and academically convincing exposé outlining the doctoral research project; it is not possible to submit revised documentation in the course of the application process or to resubmit an application following a rejection. - Previous holders of scholarships from the Studienstiftung or the Max Weber Programme, Bavaria, may apply for a scholarship to participate in the academic programmes for doctoral students, without taking advantage of financial support for their Ph. D. The selection procedure is the same for all applicants. If another funding body has awarded funding for the doctoral thesis, it must be terminated before the start of the Studienstiftung funding; otherwise the offer will expire. - Part-time doctoral degrees cannot be funded. During the period of the scholarship, Ph. D. candidates commit to concentrate fully on their dissertation; parallel study on a taught degree or professional training programme (for example for lawyers, teachers, psychologists/psychotherapists) is not compatible with the scholarship. - Doctoral studies undertaken parallel to a medical degree are not eligible for the doctoral scholarship programme; support is offered for these projects within the framework of scholarships offered by the Studienstiftung for taught degree programmes. - Funding for the completion of doctoral dissertations is not the primary focus of our doctoral scholarship programme. Applications submitted at an advanced stage will therefore generally not be considered. Applications may be included into our selection process in cases where substantial preparatory work is demonstrably required to ensure that the doctorate can be pursued on a full-time basis and completed within the regular funding period, which is typically three years. - Previous membership of the Studienstiftung is not a requirement for the doctoral programme. Selection criteria - an ambitious and innovative Ph. D. proposal, which can be completed within a three-year funding period; - effective supervision appropriate to the project and the doctoral candidate; - excellent degree results obtained within the regulation period of the study on the respective degree programme; - evidence of broad extra-curricular interests and an active commitment and engagement with society at large. For more information on the goals and support offered by the Studienstiftung, please see our Mission Statement. At a glance: Application and selection process (in German, PDF, 0.2 MB) Everyone involved in the application and selection process must agree with the principles of good scientific practice as fixed by their university resp. by the “Guidelines for Safeguarding Good Scientific Practice” of the German Research Foundation (DFG). We expect that the exposé of the proposed PhD project is based on the applicant’s authorship. Application process The number of applications for doctoral scholarships has increased significantly since 2024. To ensure applications continue to be processed within an appropriate timeframe, the Studienstiftung is adjusting its application procedure: From now on, we will publish calls for applications approximately eight months before each selection meeting. Each call remains open for two weeks (exactly 14 days). There is no limit to the number of applications we accept. All complete applications received during a call will be included in the selection procedure. Please send us your complete application documents via mail. Please send us your application documents printed on one side only and without staples, paper clips, folders, or similar items. Our mailing address is: Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes Team Promotionsförderung Ahrstr. 41 53175 Bonn Our new application procedure provides full transparency and planning reliability for applicants while making the workload more predictable for reviewers and members of the selection committee. Pre‑selection assessment takes place in two stages. During pre‑selection the office checks whether formal requirements and the Studienstiftung’s eligibility criteria are met. These include, among others: - an outstanding and highly successful academic record with strong commitment, - the support of the proposed supervisor, - a broad horizon of interests and evidence of engagement beyond one’s own academic career. At this stage the dissertation proposal (exposé) is not included in the assessment. Review process The review procedure consists of two stages: A preliminary review of applications is conducted by the Studienstiftung according to the formal application requirements listed above. At this stage, the exposé of the doctoral thesis is not taken into consideration. If an application is successful in the initial selection, it proceeds to the academic review stage. Two qualified academics provide written reviews of the applicant’s academic and extracurricular qualifications, as well as the quality of the proposed research project. First reviewers will be familiar with the applicant’s field of research. They formulate their reviews on the basis of the application documents and, in particular, consider in their review his / her academic qualifications, as well as the exposé of the proposed dissertation project. For the second review, an interview with the candidate is held in addition to the assessment of the written documents. Reviewers appointed for this review are familiar with the applicant’s discipline and have an established relationship with the Studienstiftung; they may not necessarily be experts in the candidate’s narrow research subfield. The second review evaluates the academic aspects of the application and takes the applicant's personal qualities and extra-curricular activities into consideration. To minimise travel time and costs, selection interviews are usually conducted via video calls. With mutual agreement, they may be held in person if travel time is under two hours. Applicants who face special challenges in completing the selection interview due to a disability or chronic illness should contact Dr Anne‑Sophie Käsbauer from the doctoral team after successful pre‑selection. We will then endeavour to ensure you can participate in the interview without difficulty. The selection process follows the calls’ schedule; applications are normally decided within the respective call. At a glance: General guidelines for the selection process (in German, PDF, 0.1 MB) Application documents To minimise processing times, we ask you to provide us with all the necessary documents when you submit your application. Please name four or more academics eligible to function as academic reviewers. Please note, only complete applications will be considered in the selection process. We require the following documents: - the completed application form (cover sheet for application) (in German, (DOC, 2.1 MB / PDF, 0.1 MB); - the questionnaire for applicants (in German, DOC, 2.1 MB / PDF, 0.3 MB); - the questionnaire for the doctoral supervisor (in German, DOC, 0.1 MB / PDF, 0.2 MB); - the declaration on security-relevant aspects of the research project for non-EU Applicants (PDF, 0.2 MB); - optional, the English questionnaire for the doctoral supervisor (DOC, 0.1 MB / PDF, 0.1 MB); - if available, a copy of the supervision or PhD agreement; - the applicant’s CV in both chronological and narrative formats; - a reliable ranking/an informative classification (typically an ECTS ranking or an ECTS grade classification table) for the degree that qualifies the holder for the intended PhD program; - if you apply before graduating, please submit a ranking for the academic performance to date; - a copy of the applicant's final degree certificate (MA, MSc., Diplom, Staatsexamen etc.) or a provisional certificate with a full transcript listing the results of all assessed coursework, examinations and the qualifying thesis, as well as the weighted overall degree classification; - if available, a copy of the applicant’s bachelor’s degree certificate with a full transcript listing the results of all assessed coursework, examinations and the qualifying thesis, as well as the weighted overall degree classification; - a summary of the last qualifying thesis (MA-thesis) (insofar as a final project is required in the respective subject) – no more than two or three pages; - an exposé of the proposed PhD project (German or English; maximum 20 pages), including the following information: - working title - abstract (approx. half a page) - key terms outlining the area of study - introduction - research question / problem / hypothesis - research status/ own preparatory work - methods / provisional structure - schedule and work plan - reference list and / or archival or other sources to be used - Proposal form for academic reviewer (in German, PDF, 1.5 MB) Details concerning the eligibility of potential reviewers and the Studienstiftung’s regulations on impartiality and confidentiality can be found on the proposal form. Reviewers will be contacted by the Studienstiftung; neither the doctoral supervisor nor the doctoral candidate should contact proposed reviewers beforehand. Application document checklist to download (in German, DOC, 2.1 MB). Due to the volume of applications, we are unable to return documents for unsuccessful candidates. Notifications Applicants will receive a short email informing them whether their application could be included in the selection process or whether the call had already closed. Please note that only complete applications will be considered. After pre-selection (approx. six weeks), you will be informed whether your application has been admitted to the main selection round. References for your application will then be requested. You will be informed once these reviews are received. After the selection board meeting for doctoral candidates, you will be notified of the decision on your application. Please note, the Studienstiftung cannot provide individual feedback on the decision.
