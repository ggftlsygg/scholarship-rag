Federation member alumni bursary
https://www.london.ac.uk/study/fees-funding/funding-study/scholarships-bursaries/federation-member-alumni-bursary

Federation Member Alumni bursaries are not available to alumni of every federation member institution of the University of London. Federation Member Alumni bursaries are only available if you have studied at one of the following University of London Federation Members: City, Goldsmiths, Queen Mary, School of Advanced Studies, University of London or University of London in Paris. Application deadline You must submit your request for this bursary either on your application form or via the Enquiry Hub at least 2 weeks before the registration deadline for your programme.
