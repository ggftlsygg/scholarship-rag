Embedded Files Scholarship: The Sparkling Ice® Flavorful Futures Scholarship Description: Calling all high school seniors! The Sparkling Ice® Flavorful Futures Scholarship program is now accepting applications. Follow your dreams and fund your flavorful future with a $5,000 scholarship that can be applied to any trade, vocational, or academic 2-year or 4-year program. The Flavorful Futures Scholarship is open to high school seniors who are pursuing higher education and using their passion to add flavor to their communities and beyond. Select one of the designated focus pillars, or create your own. Tell us how the pillar reflects your passion and how a scholarship can help you achieve your goals for the future. Tell us your passion and which pillar your Flavorful Future focuses on! The Pillars are: - Sustainability Initiatives - Diversity and Inclusion - Community Involvement - Other – Tell us your unique passion and how it will drive your Flavorful Future. We believe in your dreams. Cheers to the sparkle you bring to the world as you continue to discover and build on your strengths, bring your passions to life, and inspire others along the way. We can’t wait to hear about your plans for a Flavorful Future! Selection Criteria: Applications must meet ALL the following criteria: - Must be a U.S. citizen or permanent legal resident of the United States. - Must be a high school senior - Must have a cumulative high school grade point average (GPA) of at least 2.5 on a 4-point scale - Must enroll in the upcoming Fall semester as a freshman or 1st-year student in part-time or full-time study at a 2-year, 4-year, college, university, trade, or vocational school. The school must be an accredited U.S. institution and listed on the official website of the U.S. Department of Education. - Must not be a relative or dependent child of an employee of Talking Rain or any of its retail customers. Items to gather and details to consider before you apply: - Calculate your GPA. - Read the Eligibility Criteria. - Read the Terms and Conditions. - Create an original video that shows us who you are in 3 minutes or less.
