Italian government grants (typically €900/month plus fee exemptions and insurance) for international students in Master's, PhD, AFAM arts programmes and research projects at Italian institutions. What the scholarship covers This award typically covers: €900 monthly allowance (paid quarterly); Tuition/enrolment fee exemption at most institutions; Health and accident insurance; 6- or 9-month awards, renewable for multi-year courses. Read the value carefully — "full" funding can still leave gaps (visa fees, travel, or a partner/family), while a partial award may need topping up from savings or part-time work to satisfy a visa's proof-of-funds rule. Who is eligible The main requirements are strict gates rather than guidelines: A completed first degree (Bachelor's or equivalent) is required; MAECI applies age caps: 28 for Master's-level study, 30 for PhD; The annual call lists the eligible countries — check the Study in Italy portal.
