Horatio Alger National Scholarship
https://www.cltcc.edu/financial-aid/horatio-alger-scholarship

Horatio Alger National Career and Technical Scholarship Program Students can receive up to $2,500 to pursue career or technical education. Eligibility Requirements: - Have completed high school (or earned high school equivalency credential) - Be enrolled in a 2-year or less career or technical degree/certificate program that will lead to employment - Attend a not-for-profit post-secondary institution - Demonstrate financial need; applicants must be eligible to receive the Federal Pell grant - Be under the age of 30 - Be a United States Citizen To learn more about the scholarship, eligibility requirements, or to apply online, visit scholars.horatioalger.org.
