Hurford Programs is one of the best youth exchange programs, where selected participants get in touch with global networks, democracy movements, and youth movements from all around the world. The main aim of the Hurford youth fellowship is to train potential young democracy activists and to build their leadership skills. The program brings young leaders from around the world to Washington, D.C., to work on projects, exchange ideas, and develop practical solutions to strengthen democracy and youth participation in decision-making. The Hurford Youth Leadership Program is fully funded opportunity in the USA. All the expenses of the selected participants will be covered by the organization. Each fellow will receive a monthly stipend for living expenses, basic health insurance, and roundtrip travel to and from Washington, D.C. The fellows will have their own office including access to the internet and email. The program will also provide a stipend for long-distance phone calls.
