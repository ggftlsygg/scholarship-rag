Krishnan-Ang Studentships are funded by generous donations from Eashwar Viswanathan Krishnan and Tzo Tze Ang who were both undergraduates at Trinity College: Eashwar read Natural Sciences (Matric. 1996) and Tzo Tze read Electrical & Information Sciences (Matric. 1997).
