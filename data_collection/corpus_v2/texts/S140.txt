LSA International Student Scholarships at Regents of the University of Michigan in USA
https://lsa.umich.edu/scholarships/prospective-students/international-students.html

When to Apply Students may apply after they have been admitted to LSA. Application login access becomes available after admission decisions are released for Early Decision, Early Action, and Regular Decision applicants. What does the scholarship cover? For qualified LSA students, the International Student Scholarship helps to cover the cost of attendance for Fall and Winter terms. Note: Cost of Attendance includes both direct and indirect educational expenses for one academic year (Fall and Winter semesters), including tuition, fees, housing, meals, books and supplies, transportation, loan fees, and personal expenses. Amounts and Duration The amount of each scholarship varies, depending on students' cost of attendance and available funding. All International Student Scholarships can be renewed for four consecutive years (or two years' transfer) of undergraduate study. If students continue to meet the requirements, International Student Scholarships automatically renew before each fall semester. Minimum Eligibility Criteria To qualify for an International Student Scholarship, a student must: Be a prospective international (first-year or transfer) student who is accepted into a degree program in the College of LSA for the Fall term. Plan to enroll as a full-time student (At least 12 credit hours each term). NOT be a U.S. Citizen or Permanent Resident. Application Timeline The 2027 application deadline is April 9, 11:59 pm EST. Applications are reviewed as they are submitted. Applying early increases your chances of being matched with a scholarship. All scholarship notices are sent by April 16. The LSA enrollment deadline is May 1.
