Live Más Scholarship Renewal Program Eligibility The Live Más Scholarship Renewal Program is for previous recipients who meet all of the following criteria: - Applicant must be a legal resident of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia (“D.C.”) located within the United States or D.C., or the dependent child of an active duty member of the U.S. military. - There is no age restriction for applicants of the Live Más Scholarship Renewal Program. - Applicant must have previously received a Live Más Scholarship through the Live Más Scholarship Program or the Live Más Scholarship Restaurant Employee Program and must not have reached the maximum of four (4) total awards. - Applicant must be currently enrolled and in good academic standing in an accredited post-secondary educational or master’s/graduate program located in the 50 United States or D.C., including accredited two- and four-year colleges, universities, vocational-technical schools, and trade schools. - Any awarded amounts from a previous Live Más Scholarship must have been used or substantially depleted (75% or more) by January 5, 2027, 5PM PST. - Scholarships may be renewed up to three (3) times following the initial award, until you complete your post‑secondary education. Renewal is not automatic: you must re‑apply each Program Period (if eligible). Renewal award amounts are determined each Program Period in Sponsor’s sole discretion, including by reference to the applicable scoring criteria, and may be higher or lower than your original award. Applicant must be willing to work with the Taco Bell Foundation’s Scholarship Management Company throughout the application and award process. See Terms & Conditions for complete details. For technical difficulties with the application, email our scholarship management company at tacobell@mykaleidoscope.com or click the Help button on the application page Program Overview What is the Live Más Scholarship? The Live Más Scholarship is designed for students ages 16-26 who are pursuing higher education, preparing for the workforce, and using their passion to make positive change. The Live Más Scholarship provides more than just funding: scholars gain access to mentorship, career-building resources, and a community that helps them turn ambition into lifelong opportunity. Does it cost money to apply? No, there is no fee to apply. How are recipients selected? New Applicants are evaluated holistically based on (1) their passion and how they’ve pursued it, (2) a meaningful community experience and how it has shaped them, (3) the impact they’ve made or hope to make, and (4) financial need. Other factors, including educational paths and geographic locations, may also be considered. Applications may be evaluated through human review, AI-assisted tools, or a combination of both, with final scholarship selections determined by the Taco Bell Foundation. See the Terms and Conditions for complete details. Renewal Applicants are evaluated holistically based on (1) how their passion has grown or changed since receiving their first Live Más Scholarship, (2) a community experience that has shaped them or how they show up for others, (3) the impact they’ve made or hope to make and the steps they’ve taken toward it, and (4) how the Live Más Scholarship has supported their education, passion, or personal growth. Applications may be evaluated through human review, AI-assisted tools, or a combination of both, with final scholarship selections determined by the Taco Bell Foundation. See the Terms and Conditions for complete details. Do you consider financial need? Yes. For 2027, financial need is considered as one factor for new applicants. Do I need to submit FAFSA or tax documents to apply? No. You do not need to upload FAFSA or tax documents with your application.
