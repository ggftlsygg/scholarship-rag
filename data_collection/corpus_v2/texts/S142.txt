AKO Masters Scholarship presents a fully funded scholarship opportunity to students from developing countries to study at the London School of Economics, UK.The London School of Economics (LSE) AKO Masters Scholarship aims to provide eligible students with funding that covers the costs of tuition and living expenses. In this post, we will be providing you with the necessary information required for a successful application for the AKO Masters Scholarship at LSE.AKO Masters Scholarship Summary Host Country: UK Study Abroad: Study in UK Category: Postgraduate Scholarships | Masters Scholarships Eligible Countries: Developing Countries Reward: Full Scholarship | Living Stipends Deadline: April 25, AKO Masters Scholarship Details: The AKO Masters Scholarship is offered by the Department of Psychological and Behavioural Science (PBS) at the London School of Economics and Political Science. The department is offering full (fees and a stipend) and tuition fees only scholarships in the /25 academic year, generously funded by the AKO Foundation. The programmes offered at LSE enable students to learn the core theories of social psychology and behavioural science, specialise in specific areas of interest, and conduct their own research. Requirements for AKO Masters Scholarship To be considered for the AKO Masters Scholarship, you must: Demonstrate financial need. Reside in or be citizens of South Asia, Africa, or Latin America. Enroll in a one-year taught master’s program from specific PBS master’s programs listed below Scholarship applicants are expected to demonstrate how studying at LSE will enable them to contribute to societal impact activities in their home region after the successful completion of their degree at LSE. Benefits of AKO Masters Scholarship The value of the London School of Economics AKO Masters Scholarships are: Two scholarships covering the full tuition fees and providing a maintenance stipend. Eight scholarships covering the full tuition fees only.
