MBA UK/EU Bursary
https://www.london.ac.uk/study/fees-funding/funding-study/scholarships-bursaries/mba-uk/eu-bursary

Who can Apply To be eligible for this bursary you must: Be currently resident in the UK or an EU Country Have an offer of a place on the Global MBA (Masters of Business Administration) programme How to Apply You can select the UK/EU MBA Bursary in the ‘Scholarships and Bursaries’ section of the MBA online application form when you are applying for the programme. Your request for the Bursary will be processed once an offer of a place on the programme has been issued. Alternatively if you wish to apply for the UK/EU MBA Bursary after you have submitted your application for the programme, you can do so via our online enquiry system. Once you have received an offer of a place on the programme you can submit an enquiry and request the bursary. Deadline There is no deadline for applications and bursary requests are processed on an ongoing basis. We recommend that you apply for the bursary at least two weeks before registration closes.
