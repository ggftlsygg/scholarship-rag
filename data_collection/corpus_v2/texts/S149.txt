How to Apply for Michigan State University Scholarship 2026 - Visit the MSU Undergraduate Admissions portal with the green button below. - You must submit your application and all supporting materials by November 1, 2026. - There is no separate application for most international merit scholarships. - Your admission application serves as your scholarship application. - Students who meet the November 1 deadline will typically receive an admission decision and scholarship notification by January 15, 2027. - Applications submitted after November 1 will still be considered for any remaining awards on a rolling basis.
