Milt Pahl MBA Scholarship
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Milt Pahl MBA Scholarship. Amount: CAD 1,900. Term: Spring/Summer. Study load: Full Time or Part Time. Eligibility: Awarded annually to a Canadian citizen who has completed the first year of the MBA program and whose academic standing is in the top half of the class. Selection will be based on community or business leadership potential, and evidence of community contribution and/or achievement. Preference will be given to Alberta residents. Part-time students are eligible for this award.
