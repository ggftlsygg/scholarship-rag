Radhe Gupta International MBA Exchange Award
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Radhe Gupta International MBA Exchange Award. Amount: CAD 1,250. Term: Fall. Study load: Full Time. Application: Required. Eligibility: Awarded annually to an incoming international exchange students from India with satisfactory academis standing entering their final years of any MBA program in the Alberta School of Business. Recipients must have achieved a minimum GPA of 3.3 of MBA studies. Selection based on involvement in promoting or representing the Alberta MBA Program through extra-curricular initiatives and personal growth as demonstrated in their application.
