Refugee and Displaced Persons Scholarships
https://www.london.ac.uk/study/fees-funding/funding-study/scholarships-bursaries/refugee-displaced-persons-scholarships

Are you eligible? To be eligible for the scholarship you must: Have an offer for either the MSc Accounting and Financial Management, MSc Professional Accountancy, LLM, BSc Business Administration, BSc Marketing, MSc Project Management, BSc Computer Science, MSc Data Science, MA Global Diplomacy, MSc Cyber Security, Global MBA, LLB or MA Human Rights. Be a new student; or have become displaced in the course of your current study with the University of London. Have refugee or displaced person status evidenced by formal documentation. Priority will be given to those that do not already hold a bachelors or masters degree. How to apply Application deadline Applications will be considered on a rolling basis. Please ensure that you submit both the programme application and the scholarship application at least two weeks before the programme application deadline. Applications submitted after this time may not be considered.
