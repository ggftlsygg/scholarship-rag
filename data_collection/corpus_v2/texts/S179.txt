Royal Veterinary College Bursary
https://www.london.ac.uk/study/fees-funding/funding-study/scholarships-bursaries/royal-veterinary-college-bursary

Who can apply The bursary is available to the following Royal Veterinary College students: Those who have already completed a 240-hour individual module and now want to progress to the degree programme RVC graduates who now wish to register for the Veterinary Epidemiology and Public Health programme (Pg Cert, Pg Dip and MSc) How to apply Application deadline You must submit your request for this bursary either on your application form or via the online enquiry system at least 2 weeks before the registration deadline for your programme.
