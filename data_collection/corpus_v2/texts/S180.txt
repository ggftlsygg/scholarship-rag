Traditional Scholarship A*STAR Singapore Bachelor The Singapore International Pre-Graduate Award (SIPGA) supports short-term research attachments for top international students at A*STAR, giving you the unique opportunity to experience the vibrant scientific environment in A*STAR Research Institutes and Consortia, and work with distinguished and world-renowned researchers. Field of Study STEM courses Degree Level Bachelor, Master's Full Scholarship, Monthly stipend of S$2,000 - Must be proficient in written and spoken English. - Open to applicants from all countries. - Must be a 3rd or 4th year undergraduate or master’s student in STEM from an overseas university. - Must have or be on track to obtain at least Second-Class Upper Honours or equivalent. - Must remain matriculated at home university during the attachment. - Must have a strong interest in a research career.
