European Undergraduate Scholarship - 2027-28 The University of Bradford is pleased to offer the European Undergraduate Scholarship to students from eligible European Union countries commencing undergraduate study in the 2027/28 academic year. The scholarship reflects the University's commitment to maintaining strong educational links with Europe and provides a reduced tuition fee rate for eligible students. For 2027/28 entry, the scholarship has been designed so that eligible EU students pay a tuition fee equivalent to the maximum UK undergraduate fee plus 20%, resulting in a net annual fee of £12,060. The scholarship is awarded automatically and no separate application is required. - Attendance - Full-time - Available for - Prospective students - Entry year - 2027-28 - Study level - Undergraduate - Available scholarships - Unlimited How to apply Overview The University of Bradford is pleased to offer the European Undergraduate Scholarship to students from eligible European Union countries commencing undergraduate study in the 2027/28 academic year. Eligibility To be eligible for the European Undergraduate Scholarship, applicants must: - Be classified as an international fee-paying student. - Be ordinarily resident in an eligible European Union member state. - Hold an offer for a full-time undergraduate programme at the University of Bradford. - Be enrolling in the 2027/28 academic year. - Meet all academic and admissions requirements for their chosen programme. -
