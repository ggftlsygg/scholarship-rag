Your application will be reviewed holistically in consideration of the international scholarships below. SAT/ACT scores are not required but are preferred. If you have taken the SAT or ACT exam and feel your scores would enhance your overall profile, submit your official scores to be included in your review. Only degree-seeking international students admitted for on-campus study (not online) are considered. Fall applicants must apply and submit all required application materials by December 1 to be considered for scholarships. Scholarship offers for the fall term will be sent by late January.
