T.E. (Edward) Bate and T.D. (Terry) Freeman Graduate Award in Business
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

T.E. (Edward) Bate and T.D. (Terry) Freeman Graduate Award in Business. Amount: CAD 5,000. Term: Winter. Study load: Full Time. Eligibility: Awarded annually to a student registered full-time in the MBA. The recipient must hold an undergraduate degree in engineering and will be selected on the basis of satisfactory academic performance and leadership potential.
