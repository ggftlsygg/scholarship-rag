Scholarships For Tranfer Students Earning a scholarship for your first semester starts now. To increase your chances of an academic award, please apply for admission to Texas Tech. Click below to learn more about requirements and how to apply. | Transfer Merit Scholarships | | | | |---|---|---|---| | Scholarship Name | Qualifications | Award Amount | Renewal Requirement | |---|---|---|---| | Presidential Transfer Scholarship | 30 Transferable GPA Hours; 3.0 GPA | $3,000 per year | 3.0 GPA & 12 hours earned per term | | Proven Achievers Scholarship | 60 Transferable GPA Hours; 3.5 GPA | $4,000 per year | 3.25 GPA & 12 hours earned per term | Requirements and Deadlines: - Fall Entry Deadline: July 31 - Spring Entry Deadline: January 15 - Enrollment: Minimum 12 credit hours (9 must be TTU hours). - Duration: Renewable for up to three consecutive years (six total terms). - In-State Tuition: Non-residents awarded $1,000 or greater qualify for in-state rates. - Exclusions: AP, CLEP, or military credits do not count toward transferable hours. External Scholarships There are billions of dollars of external scholarship money available to students of all qualifications, abilities, and interests. These external scholarships may be provided by private donors, foundations, businesses and many other sources outside of Texas Tech University.
