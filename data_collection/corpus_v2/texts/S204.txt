Before applying for the Dream Together Scholarship, please go through the eligibility criteria and make sure you are eligible to apply. Admission Criteria: - Admissions decisions are grounded in the candidate’s academic achievements, potential, work experience, and personal accomplishments. - The admissions committee thoroughly assesses various factors, including records of past studies such as Grade Point Average (GPA), study plan, personal statement, and letters of recommendation. Additional consideration is given to other achievement records and specific qualifications, such as language proficiency. The candidate’s application is evaluated in the context of the level of studies completed, the quality of achievements, and the characteristics of institutions/organizations attended. - The appropriateness of the applicant’s academic and career goals, as well as the suitability of their preparation for the proposed program of studies, is carefully evaluated. - Interviews, examinations, and/or additional documents may be required from the applicant during the evaluation process. Admission Eligibility: - Former and current sport administrators or athletes: - The applicant and both parents of the applicant must be of non-Korean nationality. - Bachelor’s degree or higher (in any field). - Proficiency in both spoken and written English to participate in classes conducted entirely in English. - Demonstrated potential and passion for sport management. - Physically and mentally capable of successfully completing the program. You can read more on the official website of Dream Together Masters Program here: Preferred Qualifications: - Sport administrators from countries acknowledged in the List of Official Development Aid (ODA) recipients by the Development Assistance Committee (DAC), working for the government, National Olympic Committees, National Federations, or any other sport organizations under the government. - Athletes from nations recognized in the List of ODA recipients provided by the DAC, having participated in major international sporting events (Olympics, Asian Games, World Championships) with a recommendation from government sport organizations or National Olympic Committees. - Sport administrators from countries identified in the List of ODA recipients provided by the DAC, endorsed by government sport organizations or National Olympic Committees. In cases where a document is not re-issuable, applicants may submit a validated/attested/notarized copy from the issuing institute or a notary office. SNU does not provide a validation service, so applicants need to prepare a notarized copy in advance. - The admissions staff reserves the right to request additional documents from applicants to clarify eligibility or verify the authenticity of the submitted materials. - Original documents should be submitted. In cases where they are unavailable, copies must be authorized by the originating institution or notarized by a public notary in the country where the document was originally produced before submission. - Documents not in English or Korean will not be accepted. If in any other language, applicants must submit a notarized/certified translation (in English or Korean) completed by a public notary in the country where the document was originally produced. - Admissions offers will be rescinded if false information or unfair practices for admissions are discovered at any time during the application period. This condition applies even after admission or enrollment at SNU. Other Important Points: - Applicants expected to graduate from an undergraduate course must prove their graduation after the final results are released. Failure to do so will result in the rescindment of the admissions offer. - Admitted students to SNU are not permitted to register for any other universities in Korea that are scheduled to hold admissions for the same academic year as SNU.
