The University of Alabama offers generous scholarship opportunities for high-achieving students. Scholarships are awarded based on a range of factors, which may include academic achievement, standardized test scores, leadership, service, work experience and other accomplishments. Below you can find full automatic scholarship information for out-of-state or international, first-time freshmen and an overview of competitive scholarships they may be eligible for. Ready to apply? Visit How to Apply for Scholarships for a step-by-step guide to maximizing your scholarship opportunities. Premier Competitive Scholarships Awarded by Student Financial Aid and Scholarships Student Financial Aid and Scholarships awards this set of competitive freshman scholarships based on academics, honors, leadership, service and life experiences. - UA Competitive Admissions Scholarship — valued at $6,000 up to tuition. - UA Competitive Achievement Scholarship — Recognizes demonstrated achievements of entering freshmen, including national distinction in a student’s intended professional or academic area; valued at tuition plus housing and a $1,000/year supplemental scholarship. Important Scholarships Information Deadlines and Notifications Review the Financial Aid Timeline for information relevant to your entry term. Test Scores Students must submit test scores to be eligible for automatic scholarships. Students who do not submit a test score remain eligible for competitive scholarships. UA does not use superscores for scholarship purposes. Only official ACT composite scores or official SAT total scores submitted by the testing agency are accepted. Students who self-report test scores on their application must send official scores to be eligible for automatic merit scholarships. Please review the Admissions Test Scores page (https://admissions.ua.edu/testing-requirements/) for admissions test score requirements according to your entry term. GPA and Test Score Timelines Spring 2027 Application deadline and official score reports due by November 2. GPA considered through end of junior year. Summer/Fall 2027 Application deadline and official score reports due by December 4. GPA considered through end of junior year. Students who did not meet the priority deadline may be considered for scholarships on a funds available basis. Students may appeal for additional consideration due to extenuating circumstances — visit UA’s Scholarship Policy to learn more.
