New Start Scholarship New students enrolled in any online graduate program (online or on-campus), certificate, or micro-credential will automatically qualify and receive a complimentary first course tuition scholarship when enrolled in two or more courses their first semester. Start your degree with an extra boost—all compliments of a university that’s here to support and challenge you. Award = First course tuition Corporate Partner Employee UNA partners with numerous businesses, health systems, school districts, municipalities, professional associations, and other organizations to offer their employees a tuition scholarship. Learn More Award = Tuition scholarship Endowed UNA Scholarships Through the efforts of the University of North Alabama Foundation and the generosity of private donors, the Office of Student Financial Aid awards numerous endowed scholarships each academic year. These scholarships from UNA are offered by various colleges, departments, and offices throughout the University. Learn More Award = Amounts vary Alumni Scholarship (NEW) We are proud to offer qualifying UNA graduates a scholarship covering 15% of tuition toward an eligible graduate or micro-credential program, available online or on campus. To receive the scholarship, students must be admitted to an eligible program and verified as a UNA alum by the Student Accounts Office. Once alumni status and eligibility are confirmed, the scholarship will be applied to the student’s account. Please note: this is not stackable with any other UNA Scholarships. Award = 15% tuition scholarship LEAD School District Employee Partnership UNA offers incoming students from our Alabama school system partners the opportunity to obtain an advanced degree at a reduced cost.
