Who can apply Offer holders - you will not be considered for the UNHCR Bursary until you have received an offer for the MA Refugee Protection and Forced Migration Studies or a Postgraduate Laws programme. The UNHCR Bursary is only available to new students, you cannot be considered for the bursary as a registered student. How to Apply Bursary deadline and Terms and Conditions Please ensure to apply/request the bursary at least 2 weeks before the registration deadline for your programme. Terms and Conditions [PDF]
