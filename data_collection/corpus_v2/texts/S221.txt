Undergraduate Scholarships A tuition fees discount may be granted to students enrolled at the United Arab Emirates University (UAEU) under the “Scholarship Students” category to pursue their undergraduate studies. This discount is determined in accordance with the university’s approved policies and regulations and aligned with the Federal Higher Education Institutions Governance Resolution. Meeting the scholarship eligibility criteria does not constitute an entitlement to, or guarantee of, a scholarship. Scholarships are awarded subject to the availability of allocated scholarship seats. The discount applies to the categories, effective from the academic year 2025/2026 and onwards: - International students (enrolled from inside or outside the UAE). - Children of UAEU faculty members and staff. Note: These conditions apply to new students admitted from the academic year 2025/2026 onwards. Conditions and policies in place before this date remain applicable to students admitted earlier. The United Arab Emirates University offers this scholarship as one of its leading initiatives to attract top-performing high school graduates. This competitive scholarship aims to support academic and scientific excellence and is awarded to new students with outstanding academic performance, providing a full tuition waiver in accordance with the criteria and conditions specified below: Eligibility Conditions: - Achieving a minimum of (98%) in the General Secondary School Certificate (Grade 12) or its - Fulfilling all general admission requirements and the specific qualifications of the intended college/academic program. - Demonstrating English proficiency through one of the following approved tests with a minimum score: - TOEFL ITP: 590 points - TOEFL IBT: 105 points - TOEFL CPT: 233 points - Academic IELTS: 7.5 points - Demonstrating mathematics proficiency for all colleges (except College of Business and Economics, College of Humanities and Social Sciences, College of Education, and College of Law) by obtaining one of the following scores: - SAT-MATH: 730 points - ACT-MATH: 32 points - Demonstrating Arabic proficiency (for the College of Law only) by achieving a minimum score of (6.5) in the Al Ain Arabic Language Test. Scholarship Benefits: - A 100% discount on tuition fees only (the scholarship does not cover housing & admission application fees). Scholarship Continuation Conditions: - Maintaining a cumulative GPA of no less than (3.8) throughout the study duration at UAEU. - Registering for a minimum of (15) credit hours per regular semester. - The student must not hold another scholarship. - The duration of the scholarship must not exceed the normal semesters required for graduation according to the study plan of the academic program, unless otherwise stated. - Any change in the academic program, major, or study load, including course withdrawal, may affect the continuation of the scholarship. - The scholarship continues until graduation, provided the student complies with the mentioned conditions and terms. - The university reserves the right to revoke the scholarship in case the student commits any academic or behavioral violations that breach the integrity code or university regulations. Application Process: - Continuing Students Continuing and currently enrolled students may submit applications for a new scholarship, scholarship upgrade, or scholarship appeal after the release of grades, in accordance with the dates specified in the University Academic Calendar, through the following link. Application Deadlines: - New students: Scholarship applications must be submitted as part of the university admission application during the official and announced undergraduate admission periods via the link. - Continuing students: Scholarship applications will open after the final grades are released, in accordance with the dates specified in the University Academic Calendar via the link. Rules & Guidelines A student may be awarded only one scholarship and cannot combine more than one scholarship. The scholarship covers tuition fees only. The scholarship covers minor programs if they are included in the approved college plan. The scholarship does not cover repeated courses. The duration of benefiting from the scholarship must not exceed the number of regular semesters required for graduation according to the academic program’s study plan. The academic performance of scholarship recipients is evaluated at the end of each semester, and this evaluation is a condition for the continuation of the scholarship. The scholarship applies from the time it is granted; it is not retroactive, and the student is not entitled to claim it for previous academic years. No financial refund may be obtained in exchange for the tuition discount. The student is required to sign the scholarship contract before benefiting from it, and signing the contract is considered acknowledgment of full compliance with the conditions. Students enrolled in the university who meet the conditions may apply for the scholarship within a period not exceeding one academic year from their high school graduation. The university reserves the right to amend or cancel the scholarship conditions with the approval of the University’s Board of Trustees, provided that the student is notified of any changes.
