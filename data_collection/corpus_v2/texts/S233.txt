University of Miami Stamps Scholarship
https://admissions.miami.edu/undergraduate/financial-aid/scholarships/stamps/index.html

Scholarship Details The Stamps Scholarship includes tuition and fees, on-campus housing, a meal plan, University health insurance, textbooks, a laptop allowance, and access to a $12,000 enrichment fund. Enrichment fund expenses are subject to prior approval and may support study abroad, undergraduate research, unpaid internships, and other educational pursuits. The Stamps Scholarship differs from the Frost School of Music Stamps Ensemble Scholarship. To learn more about the Frost scholarship, including application requirements, visit the Frost School of Music's Scholarships and Financial Support page. Stamps Timeline Apply and be admitted under either the Early Decision I or Early Action admission plans. Semi-Finalists are contacted and selected to interview with the Stamps Scholarship Committee by mid-January. Finalists will be notified of their status as a Stamps Finalist at the time of Early Action decision release, late-January. Finalists interview with Stamps Foundation. Recipients will be announced in early-March. Scholarship Story The Stamps scholarship is the most prestigious merit award offered at the University of Miami. This premier scholarship recognizes exceptional students whose academic achievement, leadership, and curiosity position them to make the most of a University of Miami education. More than a financial award, the Stamps Scholarship gives students the opportunity to fully engage in academic, leadership, research, and global experiences during their undergraduate career. Eligibility Criteria To be considered for the Stamps Scholarship, you must apply and be admitted through either the Early Decision I or Early Action admission plans. There is no separate application outside of the Common Application to be considered. Stamps Scholars are selected through our holistic review process. Terms and Conditions A Stamps Scholar must complete a full-time undergraduate program of study with no fewer than 12 credit hours during each of the fall and spring semesters for a minimum of 24 credit hours per academic year. A minimum of a 3.0 GPA for the academic year must be maintained at the University of Miami, with the initial calculation at the end of the second semester of attendance. The award is subject to revision pending receipt of any grant, scholarship, or prepaid tuition and/or housing aid not anticipated at the time the award is made.
