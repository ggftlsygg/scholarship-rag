The University of Science and Technology of China (USTC), founded in Beijing in September 1958 by the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS) under scientific legends Guo Moruo, Qian Xuesen, Yan Jici, and Hua Luogeng, relocated to Hefei, Anhui Province in 1970. As a founding pillar of China's elite C9 League and a designated Double First-Class National University, USTC was created with the strategic mission of cultivating top-tier talent in frontier science and strategic high-technology to meet national defense and modernization requirements. USTC stands at the pinnacle of global scientific discovery, ranked #2 worldwide in the Nature Index for natural sciences output and holding the highest per-faculty research citation count across mainland China. The university is home to groundbreaking national facilities, including the National Synchrotron Radiation Laboratory (NSRL), the Hefei National Laboratory for Physical Sciences at the Microscale (HFNL), and the CAS Center for Excellence in Quantum Information and Quantum Physics. Spearheaded by world-renowned academician Pan Jianwei, USTC scientists successfully engineered the world's first quantum communications satellite ('Micius') and achieved quantum computational supremacy with the 'Jiuzhang' photonic and 'Zuchongzhi' superconducting quantum computers. Through its International College, USTC offers an expansive array of 100% English-taught Master's and Doctoral programs spanning quantum information, artificial intelligence, nanomaterials, and green energy across its East, West, Central, South, and High-Tech Gaoxin campuses, along with the Suzhou Institute of Advanced Research (SIAR). International scholars enjoy comprehensive funding through the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC Type B - Agency Code 10358), the institutional USTC Fellowship (Level A and Level B), and the ANSO Scholarship for Young Talents, with doctoral stipends reaching up to 7,000 RMB monthly. Q: What is USTC's official Agency Code for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC Type B)? USTC's official Agency Code for the Chinese Government Scholarship (CSC Type B) is 10358. When completing your application on the official CSC portal (campuschina.org), select 'Program Category Type B' and enter Agency Code 10358 to ensure your application dossier is routed directly to the USTC International College admissions committee in Hefei. Q: Do I need to secure an acceptance letter from a USTC professor before applying? No, finding a host supervisor in advance is not mandatory at USTC. Unlike many other Chinese universities, USTC's International College allows prospective international students to submit their complete online application directly through the USTC system (isa.ustc.edu.cn). Once submitted, the admissions office coordinates with relevant academic departments and professors to evaluate candidate dossiers and arrange academic interviews. However, if you already have contact with a professor who agrees to supervise you, uploading a provisional acceptance form is welcome and strengthens your profile. Q: Can I apply for English-taught graduate programs at USTC without IELTS or TOEFL? Yes. USTC accepts an official English Proficiency Certificate (EPC) issued by your previous university certifying that your undergraduate or postgraduate degree was taught and evaluated entirely in English. Submitting an EPC waives the requirement for standard standardized tests such as IELTS or TOEFL, giving qualified applicants a straightforward admissions pathway. Q: What is the difference between USTC Fellowship Level A and Level B? USTC Fellowship Level A provides comprehensive full funding matching or exceeding the CSC standard, including a 100% tuition waiver, free on-campus international student accommodation, medical insurance, and a monthly living allowance of up to 3,000 RMB for Master's students and up to 7,000 RMB for Doctoral candidates. USTC Fellowship Level B is a partial scholarship that covers full tuition and medical insurance, but requires recipients to cover their own accommodation and personal living costs. Q: What is the ANSO Scholarship for Young Talents at USTC? The ANSO Scholarship for Young Talents is a prestigious international fellowship funded by the Alliance of International Science Organizations (ANSO) in partnership with the Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). Hosted at USTC, it supports up to 200 Master's and 300 Doctoral students annually from developing nations and global partners, offering full tuition waiver, monthly stipends (3,000 RMB for Master's, 6,000–7,000 RMB for PhD), health insurance, and round-trip international travel allowance. Q: What are the key application deadlines for USTC international graduate scholarships? Applications for the Autumn (Fall) intake at USTC generally open on October 16 and close on January 31 for full scholarship consideration (CSC Type B, USTC Fellowship Level A, and ANSO). Academic departments conduct online technical interviews and document assessments between February and April, with official admission notices and JW202 visa forms dispatched between June and July.
