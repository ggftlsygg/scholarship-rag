UWA Global Excellence Scholarship [F19/1756] The University of Western Australia ('the University') provides a fee discount scholarship to encourage and assist eligible high-achieving international students to commence and pursue an undergraduate or postgraduate qualification at the University. Value: Undergraduate study: 20% discount on tuition fees (90+ ATAR); 10% discount on tuition fees (85-89.95 ATAR). Postgraduate study: 20% discount on tuition fees (75+ WAM); 10% discount on tuition fees (65-74.99 WAM). An equivalent ATAR or WAM is determined by UWA Admissions having regard to the previous study used to gain entry into the University. Equivalent scores may vary occasionally. Duration of scholarship: The maximum duration of the scholarship is for the standard full-time duration of the recipient's accepted course for which the scholarship is offered. Where an awardee receives an offer of a place into an undergraduate degree course, including an assured pathway into a postgraduate degree course, the duration of the award is for the undergraduate degree course only. Where an awardee receives an offer of a place into a combined undergraduate and master's degree course, the scholarship duration is for the combined standard full-time duration of the whole course. Concurrent scholarships: The scholarship may not be held concurrently with any other UWA issued tuition fee reduction, academic scholarship or award, with the exception of the UWA English Language Award. Eligibility requirements: To be eligible to be considered for the scholarship, applicants must: - be an international student (a student eligible for or holding an Australian student visa (subclass 500) or other visa category with study rights); - have applied to commence an eligible course specified in Schedule 1 (other structured courses of study offered by the University that do not lead to a qualification defined by the Australian Qualifications Framework are not considered an eligible course); - have achieved an ATAR of at least 85.00 if commencing undergraduate study, or have achieved a WAM of at least 65 if commencing postgraduate study (an equivalent ATAR or WAM is determined by UWA Admissions having regard to the previous study used to enter the University); - have not discontinued an equivalent level course, with an immediate break in enrolment after discontinuation. Eligibility to take up: The scholarship must be taken up in the semester for which it is awarded. To be eligible to take up the scholarship, an awardee must be enrolled: - full-time unless the administration body is satisfied that there are exceptional circumstances which preclude full-time enrolment (exceptional circumstances include, but are not limited to, a medical condition or major family commitments); - as an international student on an Australian student visa (subclass 500) or other visa category with study rights (if a student is studying offshore under an arrangement permitted by the Australian Commonwealth Government, this does not apply); and - in the course for which the scholarship is awarded in accordance with Schedule 1. Selection criterion: Academic achievement, determined by the applicants' equivalent ATAR or WAM as determined by the University. In exceptional circumstances, the University may award the scholarship based on a predicted ATAR equivalent score. Criteria for continuation: The recipient must continue to meet the eligibility requirements; make satisfactory progress in accordance with the rules for the course; maintain a Weighted Average Mark (WAM) of 60 in the course for which the scholarship is awarded; and confirm twice annually that they continue to meet the eligibility requirements of the scholarship. Application procedure: Eligible applicants to the University will be automatically considered for the scholarship and do not need to submit a separate application. The offer of the scholarship must be accepted within 30 days of the offer being made. Eligible courses (Schedule 1): Eligible Combined Bachelor's and Master's degree courses include all combined Bachelor's and Master's courses offered by the University and published in the UWA Handbook. Period of funding: 2027-2029.
