Who can Apply To be eligible for this scholarship you must: Have an offer for the programme you applied for. The programme being undertaken must be a full undergraduate or postgraduate degree. Be a new student Reside in one the following countries: Denmark, Latvia, Slovenia, Poland, Greece, Malta, Germany, Bulgaria, Portugal, Slovakia, Hungary, Czechia, Croatia, Montenegro, Serbia, North Macedonia, Turkiye, Italy and Romania. How to apply Deadlines and duties of scholarship holders Deadline for entry Applications for the 2026/7 academic year are now closed and we are not currently accepting new submissions. Applications are considered on a first come, first served basis. Once you have submitted a scholarship application we will generally aim to notify you of the result within two weeks. Duties of scholarship holders Each year you will be required to take the minimum number of examinations permitted for your programme. You should refer to your programme regulations for further information on examinations. As a scholarship holder, you are required to write a short report each year outlining the impact of your study and research, to be shared with the University of London Philanthropy and Alumni Engagement Office. a: The Philanthropy and Alumni Engagement Office is responsible for engaging with alumni (former students) and friends of the University to encourage support for scholarships. b: The Philanthropy and Alumni Engagement Office may contact you during the course of and following your studies to ask for feedback on your course and the impact of your scholarship. This feedback and your annual report will be used to report back to donors on the impact of scholarships. c. The Philanthropy and Alumni Engagement Office may also ask for your permission to use these statements (in part or in full) in future marketing and fundraising materials - you are perfectly within your rights to remain anonymous. You must also follow the standards expected of University of London students. We reserve the right to withdraw your scholarship if you do not comply with these terms.
