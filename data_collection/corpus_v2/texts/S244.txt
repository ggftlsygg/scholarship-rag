Westbury MBA Award in Philanthropy
https://www.ualberta.ca/en/business/programs/mba/admissions/awards-financial-aid/index.html

Westbury MBA Award in Philanthropy. Amount: CAD 2,250. Term: Winter. Study load: Full Time or Part Time. Application: Required. Eligibility: Awarded annually to a student registered in year one or year two of a Master of Business Administration degree program. Selection will be based on satisfactory academic standing ( equivalent to a grade point average of 3.3 or greater on the University of Alberta grading scale) and has demonstrated an interest in working in the non-profit sector and who is interested in acquiring business management skills with the intent of returning to that industry. Part-time students are eligible for this scholarship.
